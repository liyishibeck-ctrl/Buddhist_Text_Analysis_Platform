Evaṁ me sutaṁ ||| 如是我闻。
ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍige ārāme. ||| 一时，佛住舍卫城祇树给孤独园。

Tatra kho bhagavā bhikkhū āmantesi: "Ariyaṁ vo, bhikkhave, aṭṭhaṅgikaṁ maggaṁ desessāmi. Taṁ suṇātha, sādhukaṁ manasi karotha, bhāsissāmī" ti. "Evaṁ, bhante" ti kho te bhikkhū bhagavato paccassosuṁ. Bhagavā etad avoca: |||
佛告比丘：「我今当为汝说八圣道，汝善听之。」比丘应诺：「唯然，世尊。」佛如是说：

Katamo ca, bhikkhave, ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo? Seyyathidaṁ: |||
何等为**八圣道**？谓：

Sammādiṭṭhi ||| 正见
sammāsaṅkappo ||| 正思惟
sammāvācā ||| 正语
sammākammanto ||| 正业
sammāājīvo ||| 正命
sammāvāyāmo ||| 正精进
sammāsati ||| 正念
sammāsamādhi ||| 正定.

Katamā ca, bhikkhave, sammādiṭṭhi? Yā kho, bhikkhave, dukkhe ñāṇaṁ, dukkhasamudaye ñāṇaṁ, dukkhanirodhe ñāṇaṁ, dukkhanirodhagāminiyā paṭipadāya ñāṇaṁ. Ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, sammādiṭṭhi. |||
何等为正见？谓知苦，知苦集，知苦灭，知苦灭道，是名正见。

Katamo ca, bhikkhave, sammāsaṅkappo? Kāmasaṅkappaṁ, byāpādasaṅkappaṁ, vihiṁsasaṅkappaṁ. Natthi, bhikkhave, añño tato sammāsaṅkappo, yo vo, bhikkhave, akāmasaṅkappe byāpādavihiṁsasaṅkappe appahīyamāne, vikkhambhanappahānaṁ vikkhambheti, yo ca nekkhammasaṅkappo, so bhikkhave, sammāsaṅkappo. Ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, sammāsaṅkappo. |||
何等为正思惟？离欲、离瞋、离害，出要思惟，是名正思惟。

Katamā ca, bhikkhave, sammāvācā? Musāvādā veramaṇī, pisuṇāya vācāya veramaṇī, pharusāya vācāya veramaṇī, samphappalāpā veramaṇī. Yā kho, bhikkhave, musāvādā paṭivirati, pisuṇā vācā paṭivirati, pharusā vācā paṭivirati, samphappalāpā paṭivirati, yā ca ariyā aviparītā sāmaṅgī anekā, ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, sammāvācā. |||
何等为正语？离妄语，离两舌，离恶口，离绮语，是名正语。

Katamo ca, bhikkhave, sammākammanto? Pāṇātipātā veramaṇī, adinnādānā veramaṇī, abrahmacariyā veramaṇī. Yā kho, bhikkhave, pāṇātipātā paṭivirati, adinnādānā paṭivirati, abrahmacariyā paṭivirati, ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, sammākammanto. |||
何等为正业？离杀生，离不与取，离非梵行，是名正业。

Katamo ca, bhikkhave, sammāājīvo? Yā kho, bhikkhave, micchāājīvā pahāya, sammāājīvena jīvikaṁ kappeti. Ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, sammāājīvo. |||
何等为正命？舍邪命，以正命活命，是名正命。

Katamo ca, bhikkhave, sammāvāyāmo? Kāyaduccaritaṁ pahāya, kāyasucaritaṁ bhāveti, vacīduccaritaṁ pahāya, vacīsucaritaṁ bhāveti, manoduccaritaṁ pahāya, manosucaritaṁ bhāveti, idha so vāyamati, idha ussahati, idha cittaṁ paggaṇhāti, idha vaṭṭati. Ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, sammāvāyāmo. |||
何等为正精进？舍身不善，起身善，舍口不善，起口善，舍意不善，起意善，精进勤修，是名正精进。

Katamā ca, bhikkhave, sammāsati? Yā kho, bhikkhave, ajjhattaṁ kāye kāyānupassī viharati ātāpī sampajāno satimā, vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassaṁ; similarly vedanāsu ... similarly citte ... similarly dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati ātāpī sampajāno satimā, vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassaṁ. Ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, sammāsati. |||
何等为正念？谓身念住，受念住，心念住，法念住，观身不净，观受是苦，观心无常，观法无我，是名正念。

Katamaṁ ca, bhikkhave, sammāsamādhi? Yā kho, bhikkhave, vivicceva kāmehi vivicca akusalehi dhammehi savitakkaṁ savicāraṁ vivekajaṁ pīti-sukhaṁ paṭhamaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja viharati; vitakka-vicārānaṁ vūpasamā ajjhattaṁ sampasadanaṁ cetaso ekodi-bhāvaṁ avitakkaṁ avicāraṁ samādhijaṁ pīti-sukhaṁ dutiyaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja viharati; pītiyā ca virāgā upekkhako viharati satimā sampajāno, sukhañca kāyena paṭisaṁvedeti, yaṁ ariyā ācikkhanti: "upekkhako satimā sukhavihārī" ti tatiyaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja viharati; sukhassa ca pahānā dukkhassa ca pahānā, pubbeva somanassa-domanassānaṁ atthaṅgamā, adukkhaṁ asukhaṁ upekkhāsatipārisuddhiṁ catutthaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja viharati. Ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, sammāsamādhi. |||
何等为正定？初禅离欲，有寻有伺，离生喜乐；二禅无寻无伺，定生喜乐；三禅离喜妙乐，舍念清净；四禅苦乐俱灭，不苦不乐，舍念清净。是名正定。

Evaṁ sammādiṭṭhiṁ bhāveti, sammāsaṅkappaṁ bhāveti, sammāvācaṁ bhāveti, sammākammantaṁ bhāveti, sammāājīvaṁ bhāveti, sammāvāyāmaṁ bhāveti, sammāsatiṁ bhāveti, sammāsamādhiṁ bhāveti. So evaṁ bhāvento evaṁ vaḍḍhento, sabbaṁ taṇhaṁ parikkhayā parinibbāyati. |||
修习如是八圣道，渐渐增长，灭尽一切爱欲，得般涅槃。

Idamavoca bhagavā. Attamanā te bhikkhū bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti. |||
佛说此法已，比丘欢喜信受。

Iti bhagavā imaṁ suttaṁ desesi. |||
此即八圣道经。
