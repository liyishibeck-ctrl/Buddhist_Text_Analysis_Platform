Aṅguttara Nikāya 3.85 ||| Numbered Discourses 3.85
9. Samaṇavagga ||| 9. Ascetics
Sekkhasutta ||| A Trainee
Atha kho aññataro bhikkhu yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi. Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho so bhikkhu bhagavantaṁ etadavoca: ||| Then a mendicant went up to the Buddha, bowed, sat down to one side, and said to him:
“‘Sekho, sekho’ti, bhante, vuccati. ||| “Sir, they speak of the one called ‘a trainee’.
Kittāvatā nu kho, bhante, sekho hotī”ti? ||| How is a trainee defined?”
“Sikkhatīti kho, bhikkhu, tasmā sekhoti vuccati. ||| “They train, mendicant, that’s why they’re called ‘a trainee’.
Kiñca sikkhati? ||| What is their training?
Adhisīlampi sikkhati, adhicittampi sikkhati, adhipaññampi sikkhati. ||| They train in the higher ethics, the higher mind, and the higher wisdom.
Sikkhatīti kho, bhikkhu, tasmā sekhoti vuccatīti. ||| They train, that’s why they’re called ‘a trainee’.
Sekhassa sikkhamānassa, ||| As a trainee trains,
ujumaggānusārino; ||| following the straight road,
Khayasmiṁ paṭhamaṁ ñāṇaṁ, ||| first they know about ending;
tato aññā anantarā. ||| enlightenment follows in the same lifetime.
Tato aññāvimuttassa, ||| Then the knowledge comes
ñāṇaṁ ve hoti tādino; ||| such a one, freed through enlightenment,
Akuppā me vimuttīti, ||| with the end of the fetters of continued existence:
bhavasaṁyojanakkhaye”ti. ||| ‘My freedom is unshakable.’”
Pañcamaṁ.
