Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.61 ||| Linked Discourses 1.61
7. Addhavagga ||| 7. Overcome
Nāmasutta ||| Name
“Kiṁsu sabbaṁ addhabhavi, ||| “What overcomes everything?
kismā bhiyyo na vijjati; ||| What is it that nothing excels?
Kissassu ekadhammassa, ||| What is the one thing
sabbeva vasamanvagū”ti. ||| that has everything under its sway?”
“Nāmaṁ sabbaṁ addhabhavi, ||| “Name overcomes everything.
nāmā bhiyyo na vijjati; ||| Nothing excels name.
Nāmassa ekadhammassa, ||| Name is the one thing
sabbeva vasamanvagū”ti. ||| that has everything under its sway.”
