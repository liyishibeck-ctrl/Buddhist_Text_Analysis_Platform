Aṅguttara Nikāya 7.11 ||| Numbered Discourses 7.11
2. Anusayavagga ||| 2. Tendencies
Paṭhamaanusayasutta ||| Underlying Tendencies (1st)
“Sattime, bhikkhave, anusayā. ||| “Mendicants, there are these seven underlying tendencies.
Katame satta? ||| What seven?
Kāmarāgānusayo, paṭighānusayo, diṭṭhānusayo, vicikicchānusayo, mānānusayo, bhavarāgānusayo, avijjānusayo. ||| The underlying tendencies of sensual desire, aversion, views, doubt, conceit, desire for continued existence, and ignorance.
Ime kho, bhikkhave, satta anusayā”ti. ||| These are the seven underlying tendencies.”
Paṭhamaṁ.
