Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.152 ||| Numbered Discourses 4.152
16. Indriyavagga ||| 16. Faculties
Saddhābalasutta ||| The Power of Faith
“Cattārimāni, bhikkhave, balāni. ||| “Mendicants, there are these four powers.
Katamāni cattāri? ||| What four?
Saddhābalaṁ, vīriyabalaṁ, satibalaṁ, samādhibalaṁ— ||| The powers of faith, energy, mindfulness, and immersion.
Imāni kho, bhikkhave, cattāri balānī”ti. ||| These are the four powers.”
Dutiyaṁ.
