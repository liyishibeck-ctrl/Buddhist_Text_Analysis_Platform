Saṁyutta Nikāya 45.17 ||| Linked Discourses 45.17
2. Vihāravagga ||| 2. Meditation
Dutiyaparisuddhasutta ||| Purified (2nd)
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Aṭṭhime, bhikkhave, dhammā parisuddhā pariyodātā anaṅgaṇā vigatūpakkilesā anuppannā uppajjanti, nāññatra sugatavinayā. ||| “Mendicants, these eight things don’t arise to be purified, bright, flawless, and rid of corruptions apart from the Holy One’s training.
Katame aṭṭha? ||| What eight?
Seyyathidaṁ—sammādiṭṭhi …pe… sammāsamādhi. ||| They are: right view, right purpose, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, and right immersion.
Ime kho, bhikkhave, aṭṭha dhammā parisuddhā pariyodātā anaṅgaṇā vigatūpakkilesā anuppannā uppajjanti, nāññatra sugatavinayā”ti. ||| These eight things don’t arise to be purified, bright, flawless, and rid of corruptions apart from the Holy One’s training.”
Sattamaṁ.
