Saṁyutta Nikāya 36.30 ||| Linked Discourses 36.30
3. Aṭṭhasatapariyāyavagga ||| 3. The Explanation of the Hundred and Eight
Suddhikasutta ||| Plain Version
“Tisso imā, bhikkhave, vedanā. ||| “Mendicants, there are these three feelings.
Katamā tisso? ||| What three?
Sukhā vedanā, dukkhā vedanā, adukkhamasukhā vedanā— ||| Pleasant, painful, and neutral feelings.
imā kho, bhikkhave, tisso vedanā”ti. ||| These are the three feelings.”
Dasamaṁ.
