Aṅguttara Nikāya 5.173 ||| Numbered Discourses 5.173
18. Upāsakavagga ||| 18. A Lay Follower
Nirayasutta ||| Hell
“Pañcahi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato upāsako yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ niraye. ||| “Mendicants, a lay follower with five qualities is placed in hell as if delivered there.
Katamehi pañcahi? ||| What five?
Pāṇātipātī hoti …pe… surāmerayamajjapamādaṭṭhāyī hoti. ||| They kill living creatures, steal, commit sexual misconduct, lie, and consume beer, wine, and liquor intoxicants.
Imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi dhammehi samannāgato upāsako yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ niraye. ||| A lay follower with these five qualities is placed in hell as if delivered there.
Pañcahi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato upāsako yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ sagge. ||| A lay follower with five qualities is placed in heaven as if delivered there.
Katamehi pañcahi? ||| What five?
Pāṇātipātā paṭivirato hoti …pe… surāmerayamajjapamādaṭṭhānā paṭivirato hoti. ||| They don’t kill living creatures, steal, commit sexual misconduct, lie, or consume beer, wine, and liquor intoxicants.
Imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi dhammehi samannāgato upāsako yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ sagge”ti. ||| A lay follower with these five qualities is placed in heaven as if delivered there.”
Tatiyaṁ.
