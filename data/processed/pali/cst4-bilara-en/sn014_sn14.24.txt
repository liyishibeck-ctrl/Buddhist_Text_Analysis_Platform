Saṁyutta Nikāya 14.24 ||| Linked Discourses 14.24
3. Kammapathavagga ||| 3. Ways of Performing Deeds
Dussīlasutta ||| Unethical
Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Dhātusova, bhikkhave, sattā saṁsandanti samenti. ||| “Mendicants, sentient beings join together and converge because of an element:
Assaddhā assaddhehi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samenti; ||| the faithless with the faithless …
ahirikā ahirikehi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samenti; ||| shameless …
anottappino anottappīhi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samenti; ||| imprudent …
dussīlā dussīlehi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samenti; ||| unethical …
duppaññā duppaññehi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samenti. ||| witless …
Saddhā saddhehi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samenti; ||| The faithful with the faithful …
hirimanā hirimanehi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samenti; ||| conscientious …
ottappino ottappīhi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samenti; ||| prudent …
sīlavanto sīlavantehi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samenti; ||| ethical …
paññavanto paññavantehi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samentī”ti. ||| and the wise with the wise.”
Dutiyaṁ.
