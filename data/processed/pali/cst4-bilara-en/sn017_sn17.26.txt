Saṁyutta Nikāya 17.26 ||| Linked Discourses 17.26
3. Tatiyavagga ||| Chapter Three
Dutiyasamaṇabrāhmaṇasutta ||| Ascetics and Brahmins (2nd)
Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Ye hi keci, bhikkhave, samaṇā vā brāhmaṇā vā lābhasakkārasilokassa samudayañca atthaṅgamañca assādañca ādīnavañca nissaraṇañca yathābhūtaṁ nappajānanti …pe… ||| “There are ascetics and brahmins who don’t truly understand the origin, disappearance, gratification, drawback, and escape when it comes to possessions, honor, and popularity …
pajānanti …pe… ||| There are ascetics and brahmins who do truly understand …”
sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja viharantī”ti.
Chaṭṭhaṁ.
