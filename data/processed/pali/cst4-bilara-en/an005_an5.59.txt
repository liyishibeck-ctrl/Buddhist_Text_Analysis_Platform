Aṅguttara Nikāya 5.59 ||| Numbered Discourses 5.59
6. Nīvaraṇavagga ||| 6. Hindrances
Paṭhamavuḍḍhapabbajitasutta ||| Gone Forth When Old (1st)
“Pañcahi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato dullabho vuḍḍhapabbajito. ||| “Mendicants, it’s hard to find someone gone forth when old who has five qualities.
Katamehi pañcahi? ||| What five?
Dullabho, bhikkhave, vuḍḍhapabbajito nipuṇo, dullabho ākappasampanno, dullabho bahussuto, dullabho dhammakathiko, dullabho vinayadharo. ||| It’s hard to find someone gone forth when old who is sophisticated, well-presented, and learned, who can teach Dhamma, and has memorized the monastic law.
Imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi dhammehi samannāgato dullabho vuḍḍhapabbajitoti. ||| It’s hard to find someone gone forth when old who possesses these five qualities.”
Navamaṁ.
