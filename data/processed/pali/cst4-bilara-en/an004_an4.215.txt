Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.215 ||| Numbered Discourses 4.215
22. Parisāvagga ||| 22. Assembly
Paṭhamamaggasutta ||| Path (1st)
…pe… Micchādiṭṭhiko hoti, micchāsaṅkappo hoti, micchāvāco hoti, micchākammanto hoti …pe… sammādiṭṭhiko hoti, sammāsaṅkappo hoti, sammāvāco hoti, sammākammanto hoti. ||| “Someone with four qualities is placed in hell as if delivered there. … wrong view, wrong purpose, wrong speech, wrong action. … Someone with four qualities is placed in heaven as if delivered there. … right view, right purpose, right speech, right action. …”
Pañcamaṁ.
