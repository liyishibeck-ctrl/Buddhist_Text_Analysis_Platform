Dutiyābhinandasutta ||| Taking Pleasure (Exterior)
“Yo, bhikkhave, rūpe abhinandati, dukkhaṁ so abhinandati. ||| “Mendicants, if you take pleasure in sights, you take pleasure in suffering.
Yo dukkhaṁ abhinandati, ‘aparimutto so dukkhasmā’ti vadāmi. ||| If you take pleasure in suffering, you’re not free from suffering, I say. …”
