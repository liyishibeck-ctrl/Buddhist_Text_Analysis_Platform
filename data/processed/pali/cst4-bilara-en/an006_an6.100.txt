Aṅguttara Nikāya 6.100 ||| Numbered Discourses 6.100
10. Ānisaṁsavagga ||| 10. Benefit
Anattasutta ||| Not-Self
“So vata, bhikkhave, bhikkhu kañci dhammaṁ attato samanupassanto …pe… ||| “Mendicants, it is quite impossible for a mendicant who regards any condition as self to accept views that agree with the teaching. …
sabbadhamme anattato samanupassanto …pe… ||| It’s quite possible for a mendicant who regards all things as not-self to accept views that agree with the teaching. …”
ṭhānametaṁ vijjati”.
Pañcamaṁ.
