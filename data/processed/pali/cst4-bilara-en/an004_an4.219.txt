Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.219 ||| Numbered Discourses 4.219
22. Parisāvagga ||| 22. Assembly
Ahirikasutta ||| Lack of Conscience
…pe… Assaddho hoti, dussīlo hoti, ahiriko hoti, anottappī hoti …pe… saddho hoti, sīlavā hoti, hirimā hoti, ottappī hoti. ||| “Someone with four qualities is placed in hell as if delivered there. … They’re faithless, unethical, shameless, and imprudent. … Someone with four qualities is placed in heaven as if delivered there. … They’re faithful, ethical, conscientious, and prudent. …”
Navamaṁ.
