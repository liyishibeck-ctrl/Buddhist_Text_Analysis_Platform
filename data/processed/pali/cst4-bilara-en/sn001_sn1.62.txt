Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.62 ||| Linked Discourses 1.62
7. Addhavagga ||| 7. Overcome
Cittasutta ||| Mind
“Kenassu nīyati loko, ||| “What leads the world on?
kenassu parikassati; ||| What drags it around?
Kissassu ekadhammassa, ||| What is the one thing
sabbeva vasamanvagū”ti. ||| that has everything under its sway?”
“Cittena nīyati loko, ||| “The mind leads the world on.
cittena parikassati; ||| The mind drags it around.
Cittassa ekadhammassa, ||| Mind is the one thing
sabbeva vasamanvagū”ti. ||| that has everything under its sway.”
