Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.79 ||| Linked Discourses 1.79
8. Chetvāvagga ||| 8. Slain
Pātheyyasutta ||| Provisions
“Kiṁsu bandhati pātheyyaṁ, ||| “What binds the provisions for a journey?
kiṁsu bhogānamāsayo; ||| What’s the vault of wealth?
Kiṁsu naraṁ parikassati, ||| What drags a person around?
kiṁsu lokasmi dujjahaṁ; ||| What in the world is hard to give up?
Kismiṁ baddhā puthū sattā, ||| With what are ordinary beings bound,
pāsena sakuṇī yathā”ti. ||| like birds in a snare?”
“Saddhā bandhati pātheyyaṁ, ||| “Faith binds the provisions for a journey.
sirī bhogānamāsayo; ||| Majesty is the vault of wealth.
Icchā naraṁ parikassati, ||| Desire drags a person around.
icchā lokasmi dujjahā; ||| Desire in the world is hard to give up.
Icchābaddhā puthū sattā, ||| Ordinary beings are bound by desire,
pāsena sakuṇī yathā”ti. ||| like birds in a snare.”
