Saṁyutta Nikāya 14.21 ||| Linked Discourses 14.21
2. Dutiyavagga ||| Chapter Two
Appassutamūlakasutta ||| Beginning With the Unlearned
Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Dhātusova, bhikkhave, sattā saṁsandanti samenti. ||| “Mendicants, sentient beings join together and converge because of an element:
Appassutā appassutehi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samenti; ||| the unlearned with the unlearned …
kusītā kusītehi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samenti; ||| lazy …
duppaññā duppaññehi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samenti; ||| witless.
bahussutā bahussutehi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samenti; ||| The learned with the learned …
āraddhavīriyā āraddhavīriyehi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samenti; paññavanto paññavantehi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samenti …pe…. ||| energetic … wise.
Appassutā appassutehi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samenti; ||| … the unlearned with the unlearned …
muṭṭhassatino muṭṭhassatīhi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samenti; ||| unmindful …
duppaññā duppaññehi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samenti; ||| witless.
bahussutā bahussutehi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samenti; ||| The learned with the learned …
upaṭṭhitassatino upaṭṭhitassatīhi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samenti; paññavanto paññavantehi saddhiṁ saṁsandanti samentī”ti …pe…. ||| mindful … wise.”
Ekādasamaṁ.
