Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.72 ||| Linked Discourses 1.72
8. Chetvāvagga ||| 8. Slain
Rathasutta ||| A Chariot
“Kiṁsu rathassa paññāṇaṁ, ||| “What is a chariot known by?
kiṁsu paññāṇamaggino; ||| What is fire known by?
Kiṁsu raṭṭhassa paññāṇaṁ, ||| What is a nation known by?
kiṁsu paññāṇamitthiyā”ti. ||| What is a woman known by?”
“Dhajo rathassa paññāṇaṁ, ||| “A chariot is known by a banner.
dhūmo paññāṇamaggino; ||| Fire is known by smoke.
Rājā raṭṭhassa paññāṇaṁ, ||| A nation is known by its ruler.
bhattā paññāṇamitthiyā”ti. ||| A woman is known by her husband.”
