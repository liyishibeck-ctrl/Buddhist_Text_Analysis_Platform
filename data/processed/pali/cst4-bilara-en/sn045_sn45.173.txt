Saṁyutta Nikāya 45.173 ||| Linked Discourses 45.173
14. Oghavagga ||| 14. Floods
Upādānasutta ||| Grasping
“Cattārimāni, bhikkhave, upādānāni. ||| “Mendicants, there are these four kinds of grasping.
Katamāni cattāri? ||| What four?
Kāmupādānaṁ, diṭṭhupādānaṁ, sīlabbatupādānaṁ, attavādupādānaṁ— ||| Grasping at sensual pleasures, views, precepts and observances, and theories of a self.
imāni kho, bhikkhave, cattāri upādānāni. ||| These are the four kinds of grasping.
Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, catunnaṁ upādānānaṁ abhiññāya pariññāya parikkhayāya pahānāya …pe… ayaṁ ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo bhāvetabbo”ti. ||| The noble eightfold path should be developed for the direct knowledge, complete understanding, finishing, and giving up of these four kinds of grasping.”
Tatiyaṁ.
