Saṁyutta Nikāya 15.15 ||| Linked Discourses 15.15
2. Dutiyavagga ||| Chapter Two
Pitusutta ||| Father
Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Anamataggoyaṁ, bhikkhave, saṁsāro …pe… ||| “Mendicants, this transmigration has no known beginning. …
na so, bhikkhave, satto sulabharūpo yo napitābhūtapubbo …pe… ||| It’s not easy to find a sentient being who in all this long time has not previously been your father. …
alaṁ vimuccitun”ti. ||| This is quite enough for you to become disillusioned, dispassionate, and freed regarding all conditions.”
Pañcamaṁ.
