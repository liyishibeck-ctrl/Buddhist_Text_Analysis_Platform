Saṁyutta Nikāya 25.6 ||| Linked Discourses 25.6
1. Cakkhuvagga ||| 1. The Eye
Rūpasaññāsutta ||| Perception
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Rūpasaññā, bhikkhave, aniccā vipariṇāmī aññathābhāvī; ||| “Mendicants, perception of sights is impermanent, decaying, and perishing.
saddasaññā … ||| Perception of sounds,
gandhasaññā … ||| perception of smells,
rasasaññā … ||| perception of tastes,
phoṭṭhabbasaññā … ||| perception of touches,
dhammasaññā aniccā vipariṇāmī aññathābhāvī. ||| and perception of ideas are impermanent, decaying, and perishing.
Yo, bhikkhave, ime dhamme evaṁ saddahati adhimuccati, ayaṁ vuccati ‘saddhānusārī …pe… ||| Someone who has faith and confidence in these teachings is called a follower by faith. …”
sambodhiparāyano’”ti.
Chaṭṭhaṁ.
