Saṁyutta Nikāya 27.4 ||| Linked Discourses 27.4
1. Kilesavagga ||| 1. Corruptions
Samphassasutta ||| Contact
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Yo, bhikkhave, cakkhusamphassasmiṁ chandarāgo, cittasseso upakkileso. ||| “Mendicants, desire and greed for eye contact,
Yo sotasamphassasmiṁ … ||| ear contact,
yo ghānasamphassasmiṁ … ||| nose contact,
yo jivhāsamphassasmiṁ … ||| tongue contact,
yo kāyasamphassasmiṁ … ||| body contact,
yo manosamphassasmiṁ chandarāgo, cittasseso upakkileso. ||| or mind contact is a corruption of the mind. …”
Yato kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno …pe…
abhiññā sacchikaraṇīyesu dhammesū”ti.
Catutthaṁ.
