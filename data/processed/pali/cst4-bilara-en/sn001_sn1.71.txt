Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.71 ||| Linked Discourses 1.71
8. Chetvāvagga ||| 8. Slain
Chetvāsutta ||| Slain
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
Ekamantaṁ ṭhitā kho sā devatā bhagavantaṁ gāthāya ajjhabhāsi: ||| Standing to one side, that deity addressed the Buddha in verse:
“Kiṁsu chetvā sukhaṁ seti, ||| “When what is slain do you sleep at ease?
kiṁsu chetvā na socati; ||| When what is slain is there no sorrow?
Kissassu ekadhammassa, ||| What’s the one thing, Gotama,
vadhaṁ rocesi gotamā”ti. ||| whose killing you approve?”
“Kodhaṁ chetvā sukhaṁ seti, ||| “When anger’s slain you sleep at ease.
kodhaṁ chetvā na socati; ||| When anger’s slain there is no sorrow.
Kodhassa visamūlassa, ||| O deity, anger has a poisonous root
madhuraggassa devate; ||| and a sweet tip.
Vadhaṁ ariyā pasaṁsanti, ||| The noble ones praise its killing,
tañhi chetvā na socatī”ti. ||| for when it’s slain there is no sorrow.”
