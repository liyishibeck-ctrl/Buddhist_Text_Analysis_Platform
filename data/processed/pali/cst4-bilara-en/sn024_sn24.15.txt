Saṁyutta Nikāya 24.15 ||| Linked Discourses 24.15
1. Sotāpattivagga ||| 1. Stream-Entry
Hotitathāgatosutta ||| A Realized One Still Exists
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Kismiṁ nu kho, bhikkhave, sati, kiṁ upādāya, kiṁ abhinivissa evaṁ diṭṭhi uppajjati: ||| “Mendicants, when what exists, because of grasping what and insisting on what, does the view arise:
‘hoti tathāgato paraṁ maraṇā’”ti? ||| ‘A realized one still exists after death’?” …
“Bhagavaṁmūlakā no, bhante, dhammā …pe…
niyato sambodhiparāyano”ti.
Pannarasamaṁ.
