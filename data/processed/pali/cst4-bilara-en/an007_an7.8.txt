Aṅguttara Nikāya 7.8 ||| Numbered Discourses 7.8
1. Dhanavagga ||| 1. Wealth
Saṁyojanasutta ||| Fetters
“Sattimāni, bhikkhave, saṁyojanāni. ||| “Mendicants, there are these seven fetters.
Katamāni satta? ||| What seven?
Anunayasaṁyojanaṁ, paṭighasaṁyojanaṁ, diṭṭhisaṁyojanaṁ, vicikicchāsaṁyojanaṁ, mānasaṁyojanaṁ, bhavarāgasaṁyojanaṁ, avijjāsaṁyojanaṁ. ||| The fetters of attraction, aversion, views, doubt, conceit, desire for continued existence, and ignorance.
Imāni kho, bhikkhave, satta saṁyojanānī”ti. ||| These are the seven fetters.”
Aṭṭhamaṁ.
