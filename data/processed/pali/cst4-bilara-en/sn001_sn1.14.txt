Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.14 ||| Linked Discourses 1.14
2. Nandanavagga ||| 2. The Garden of Delight
Khattiyasutta ||| Aristocrats
“Khattiyo dvipadaṁ seṭṭho, ||| “An aristocrat is the best of bipeds,
balībaddo catuppadaṁ; ||| an ox is the best of quadrupeds,
Komārī seṭṭhā bhariyānaṁ, ||| a maiden is the best of wives,
yo ca puttāna pubbajo”ti. ||| and a first-born the best of sons.”
“Sambuddho dvipadaṁ seṭṭho, ||| “A Buddha is the best of bipeds,
ājānīyo catuppadaṁ; ||| a thoroughbred, the best of quadrupeds,
Sussūsā seṭṭhā bhariyānaṁ, ||| a good listener is the best of wives,
yo ca puttānamassavo”ti. ||| and the best of sons is loyal.”
