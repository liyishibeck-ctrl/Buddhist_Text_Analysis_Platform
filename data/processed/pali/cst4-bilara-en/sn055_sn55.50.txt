Saṁyutta Nikāya 55.50 ||| Linked Discourses 55.50
5. Sagāthakapuññābhisandavagga ||| 5. Overflowing Merit, With Verses
Aṅgasutta ||| Factors
“Cattārimāni, bhikkhave, sotāpattiyaṅgāni. ||| “Mendicants, there are these four factors for stream-entry.
Katamāni cattāri? ||| What four?
Sappurisasaṁsevo, saddhammassavanaṁ, yonisomanasikāro, dhammānudhammappaṭipatti— ||| Associating with true persons, listening to the true teaching, rational application of mind, and practicing in line with the teaching.
imāni kho, bhikkhave, cattāri sotāpattiyaṅgānī”ti. ||| These are the four factors for stream-entry.”
Dasamaṁ.
Sagāthakapuññābhisandavaggo pañcamo.
Tassuddānaṁ
Abhisandā tayo vuttā,
Duve mahaddhanena ca;
Suddhaṁ nandiyaṁ bhaddiyaṁ,
Mahānāmaṅgena te dasāti.
