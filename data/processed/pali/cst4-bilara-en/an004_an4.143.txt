Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.143 ||| Numbered Discourses 4.143
15. Ābhāvagga ||| 15. Brightness
Ālokasutta ||| Light
“Cattārome, bhikkhave, ālokā. ||| “Mendicants, there are these four lights.
Katame cattāro? ||| What four?
Candāloko, sūriyāloko, aggāloko, paññāloko— ||| The lights of the moon, sun, fire, and wisdom.
Ime kho, bhikkhave, cattāro ālokā. ||| These are the four lights.
Etadaggaṁ, bhikkhave, imesaṁ catunnaṁ ālokānaṁ yadidaṁ paññāloko”ti. ||| The best of these four lights is the light of wisdom.”
Tatiyaṁ.
