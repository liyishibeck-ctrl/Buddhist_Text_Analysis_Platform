Saṁyutta Nikāya 19.4 ||| Linked Discourses 19.4
1. Paṭhamavagga ||| Chapter One
Nicchavisutta ||| A Flayed Man
“Idhāhaṁ, āvuso, gijjhakūṭā pabbatā orohanto addasaṁ nicchaviṁ purisaṁ vehāsaṁ gacchantaṁ. ||| “Just now, reverend, as I was descending from Vulture’s Peak Mountain I saw a flayed man flying through the air.
Tamenaṁ gijjhāpi kākāpi kulalāpi anupatitvā anupatitvā vitacchenti virājenti. ||| Vultures, crows, and hawks kept chasing it, pecking and clawing
So sudaṁ aṭṭassaraṁ karoti …pe… ||| as he screamed in pain. …” …
eso, bhikkhave, satto imasmiṁyeva rājagahe orabbhiko ahosi …pe…. ||| “That being used to be a sheep butcher right here in Rājagaha. …”
Catutthaṁ.
