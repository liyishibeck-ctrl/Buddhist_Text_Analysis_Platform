Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.231 ||| Numbered Discourses 4.231
23. Duccaritavagga ||| 23. Bad Conduct
Kavisutta ||| Poets
“Cattārome, bhikkhave, kavī. ||| “Mendicants, there are these four poets.
Katame cattāro? ||| What four?
Cintākavi, sutakavi, atthakavi, paṭibhānakavi— ||| A poet who thoughtfully composes their own work, a poet who repeats the oral transmission, a poet who educates, and a poet who improvises.
Ime kho, bhikkhave, cattāro kavī”ti. ||| These are the four poets.”
Ekādasamaṁ.
Duccaritavaggo tatiyo.
Tassuddānaṁ
Duccaritaṁ diṭṭhi akataññū ca,
Pāṇātipātāpi dve maggā;
Dve vohārapathā vuttā,
Ahirikaṁ duppaññakavinā cāti.
