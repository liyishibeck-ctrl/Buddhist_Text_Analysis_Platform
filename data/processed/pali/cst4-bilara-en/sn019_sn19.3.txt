Saṁyutta Nikāya 19.3 ||| Linked Discourses 19.3
1. Paṭhamavagga ||| Chapter One
Piṇḍasutta ||| A Piece of Flesh
“Idhāhaṁ, āvuso, gijjhakūṭā pabbatā orohanto addasaṁ maṁsapiṇḍaṁ vehāsaṁ gacchantaṁ. ||| “Just now, reverend, as I was descending from Vulture’s Peak Mountain I saw a piece of flesh flying through the air.
Tamenaṁ gijjhāpi kākāpi kulalāpi anupatitvā anupatitvā vitacchenti virājenti. ||| Vultures, crows, and hawks kept chasing it, pecking and clawing
Sā sudaṁ aṭṭassaraṁ karoti …pe… ||| as it screeched in pain. …” …
eso, bhikkhave, satto imasmiṁyeva rājagahe sākuṇiko ahosi …pe…. ||| “That being used to be a bird hunter right here in Rājagaha. …”
Tatiyaṁ.
