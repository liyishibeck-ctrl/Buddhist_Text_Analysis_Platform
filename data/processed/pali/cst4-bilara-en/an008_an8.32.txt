Aṅguttara Nikāya 8.32 ||| Numbered Discourses 8.32
4. Dānavagga ||| 4. Giving
Dutiyadānasutta ||| Giving (2nd)
“Saddhā hiriyaṁ kusalañca dānaṁ, ||| “Faith, conscience, and skillful giving
Dhammā ete sappurisānuyātā; ||| are qualities true persons follow.
Etañhi maggaṁ diviyaṁ vadanti, ||| For this, they say, is the path of the gods,
Etena hi gacchati devalokan”ti. ||| which leads to the heavenly realm.”
Dutiyaṁ.
