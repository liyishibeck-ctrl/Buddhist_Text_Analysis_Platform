Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.69 ||| Linked Discourses 1.69
7. Addhavagga ||| 7. Overcome
Icchāsutta ||| Desire
“Kenassu bajjhatī loko, ||| “What is it that binds the world?
kissa vinayāya muccati; ||| By removing what is it freed?
Kissassu vippahānena, ||| With the giving up of what
sabbaṁ chindati bandhanan”ti. ||| are all bonds severed?”
“Icchāya bajjhatī loko, ||| “Desire is what binds the world.
icchāvinayāya muccati; ||| By the removing of desire it’s freed.
Icchāya vippahānena, ||| With the giving up of desire,
sabbaṁ chindati bandhanan”ti. ||| all bonds are severed.”
