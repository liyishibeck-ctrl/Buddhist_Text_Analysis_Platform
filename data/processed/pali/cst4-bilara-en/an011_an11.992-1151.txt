Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka ||| The First Fifty
Rāgapeyyāla ||| Abbreviated Texts Beginning with Greed

“Dosassa… pe… ||| “Of hate …
mohassa… ||| delusion …
kodhassa… ||| anger …
upanāhassa… ||| acrimony …
makkhassa… ||| disdain …
paḷāsassa… ||| contempt …
issāya… ||| jealousy …
macchariyassa… ||| stinginess …
māyāya… ||| deceitfulness …
sāṭheyyassa… ||| deviousness …
thambhassa… ||| obstinacy …
sārambhassa… ||| aggression …
mānassa… ||| conceit …
atimānassa… ||| arrogance …
madassa… ||| vanity …
pamādassa abhiññāya…pe… ||| for insight into negligence …
pariññāya… ||| complete understanding …
parikkhayāya… ||| complete ending …

khayāya… ||| ending …

Idamavoca bhagavā. ||| That is what the Buddha said.
Attamanā te bhikkhū bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti. ||| Satisfied, the mendicants approved what the Buddha said.
Rāgapeyyālaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
Nava suttasahassāni,
bhiyyo pañcasatāni ca;
Sattapaññāsa suttantā,
aṅguttarasamāyutāti.
Ekādasakanipātapāḷi niṭṭhitā. ||| The Book of the Elevens is finished.
Aṅguttaranikāyo samatto. ||| The Numbered Discourses are completed.
