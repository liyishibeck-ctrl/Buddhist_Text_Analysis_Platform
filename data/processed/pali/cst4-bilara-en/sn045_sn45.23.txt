Saṁyutta Nikāya 45.23 ||| Linked Discourses 45.23
3. Micchattavagga ||| 3. The Wrong Way
Paṭhamapaṭipadāsutta ||| Practice (1st)
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Micchāpaṭipadañca vo, bhikkhave, desessāmi, sammāpaṭipadañca. ||| “Mendicants, I will teach you the wrong practice and the right practice.
Taṁ suṇātha. ||| Listen …
Katamā ca, bhikkhave, micchāpaṭipadā? ||| And what’s the wrong practice?
Seyyathidaṁ—micchādiṭṭhi …pe… micchāsamādhi. ||| It is wrong view, wrong purpose, wrong speech, wrong action, wrong livelihood, wrong effort, wrong mindfulness, and wrong immersion.
Ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, micchāpaṭipadā. ||| This is called the wrong practice.
Katamā ca, bhikkhave, sammāpaṭipadā? ||| And what’s the right practice?
Seyyathidaṁ—sammādiṭṭhi …pe… sammāsamādhi. ||| It is right view, right purpose, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, and right immersion.
Ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, sammāpaṭipadā”ti. ||| This is called the right practice.”
Tatiyaṁ.
