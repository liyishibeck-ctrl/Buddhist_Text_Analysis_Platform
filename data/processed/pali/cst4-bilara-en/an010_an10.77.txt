Aṅguttara Nikāya 10.77 ||| Numbered Discourses 10.77
8. Ākaṅkhavagga ||| 8. If You Want
Kākasutta ||| A Crow
“Dasahi, bhikkhave, asaddhammehi samannāgato kāko. ||| “Mendicants, a crow has ten bad qualities.
Katamehi dasahi? ||| What ten?
Dhaṁsī ca, pagabbho ca, tintiṇo ca, mahagghaso ca, luddo ca, akāruṇiko ca, dubbalo ca, oravitā ca, muṭṭhassati ca, necayiko ca— ||| They’re rude and impudent, gluttonous and voracious, cruel and pitiless, weak and raucous, unmindful and acquisitive.
imehi kho, bhikkhave, dasahi asaddhammehi samannāgato kāko. ||| A crow possesses these ten bad qualities.
Evamevaṁ kho, bhikkhave, dasahi asaddhammehi samannāgato pāpabhikkhu. ||| In the same way, a bad mendicant possesses these ten bad qualities.
Katamehi dasahi? ||| What ten?
Dhaṁsī ca, pagabbho ca, tintiṇo ca, mahagghaso ca, luddo ca, akāruṇiko ca, dubbalo ca, oravitā ca, muṭṭhassati ca, necayiko ca— ||| They’re rude and impudent, gluttonous and voracious, cruel and pitiless, weak and raucous, unmindful and acquisitive.
imehi kho, bhikkhave, dasahi asaddhammehi samannāgato pāpabhikkhū”ti. ||| A bad mendicant possesses these ten bad qualities.”
Sattamaṁ.
