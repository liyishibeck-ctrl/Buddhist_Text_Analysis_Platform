Saṁyutta Nikāya 45.1 ||| Linked Discourses 45.1
1. Avijjāvagga ||| 1. Ignorance
Avijjāsutta ||| Ignorance
Evaṁ me sutaṁ— ||| So I have heard.
ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. ||| At one time the Buddha was staying near Sāvatthī in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s monastery.
Tatra kho bhagavā bhikkhū āmantesi: ||| There the Buddha addressed the mendicants,
“bhikkhavo”ti. ||| “Mendicants!”
“Bhadante”ti te bhikkhū bhagavato paccassosuṁ. ||| “Venerable sir,” they replied.
Bhagavā etadavoca: ||| The Buddha said this:
“Avijjā, bhikkhave, pubbaṅgamā akusalānaṁ dhammānaṁ samāpattiyā, anvadeva ahirikaṁ anottappaṁ. ||| “Mendicants, ignorance precedes the attainment of unskillful qualities, with lack of conscience and prudence following along.
Avijjāgatassa, bhikkhave, aviddasuno micchādiṭṭhi pahoti; ||| An ignoramus, sunk in ignorance, produces wrong view.
micchādiṭṭhissa micchāsaṅkappo pahoti; ||| Wrong view produces wrong purpose.
micchāsaṅkappassa micchāvācā pahoti; ||| Wrong purpose produces wrong speech.
micchāvācassa micchākammanto pahoti; ||| Wrong speech produces wrong action.
micchākammantassa micchāājīvo pahoti; ||| Wrong action produces wrong livelihood.
micchāājīvassa micchāvāyāmo pahoti; ||| Wrong livelihood produces wrong effort.
micchāvāyāmassa micchāsati pahoti; ||| Wrong effort produces wrong mindfulness.
micchāsatissa micchāsamādhi pahoti. ||| Wrong mindfulness produces wrong immersion.
Vijjā ca kho, bhikkhave, pubbaṅgamā kusalānaṁ dhammānaṁ samāpattiyā, anvadeva hirottappaṁ. ||| Knowledge precedes the attainment of skillful qualities, with conscience and prudence following along.
Vijjāgatassa, bhikkhave, viddasuno sammādiṭṭhi pahoti; ||| A knowing one, firm in knowledge, produces right view.
sammādiṭṭhissa sammāsaṅkappo pahoti; ||| Right view produces right purpose.
sammāsaṅkappassa sammāvācā pahoti; ||| Right purpose produces right speech.
sammāvācassa sammākammanto pahoti; ||| Right speech produces right action.
sammākammantassa sammāājīvo pahoti; ||| Right action produces right livelihood.
sammāājīvassa sammāvāyāmo pahoti; ||| Right livelihood produces right effort.
sammāvāyāmassa sammāsati pahoti; ||| Right effort produces right mindfulness.
sammāsatissa sammāsamādhi pahotī”ti. ||| Right mindfulness produces right immersion.”
Paṭhamaṁ.
