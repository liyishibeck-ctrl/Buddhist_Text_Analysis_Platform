Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.144 ||| Numbered Discourses 4.144
15. Ābhāvagga ||| 15. Brightness
Obhāsasutta ||| Shining
“Cattārome, bhikkhave, obhāsā. ||| “Mendicants, there are four kinds of shining.
Katame cattāro? ||| What four?
Candobhāso, sūriyobhāso, aggobhāso, paññobhāso— ||| The shining of the moon, sun, fire, and wisdom.
Ime kho, bhikkhave, cattāro obhāsā. ||| These are the four kinds of shining.
Etadaggaṁ, bhikkhave, imesaṁ catunnaṁ obhāsānaṁ yadidaṁ paññobhāso”ti. ||| The best of these four kinds of shining is the shining of wisdom.”
Catutthaṁ.
