Saṁyutta Nikāya 22.75 ||| Linked Discourses 22.75
8. Khajjanīyavagga ||| 8. Bitable
Dutiyasamudayasutta ||| Origin (2nd)
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Sutavā, bhikkhave, ariyasāvako rūpassa samudayañca atthaṅgamañca assādañca ādīnavañca nissaraṇañca yathābhūtaṁ pajānāti. ||| “Mendicants, a learned noble disciple truly understands the origin, the disappearance, the gratification, the drawback, and the escape when it comes to form,
Vedanāya … ||| feeling,
saññāya … ||| perception,
saṅkhārānaṁ … ||| choices,
viññāṇassa samudayañca atthaṅgamañca assādañca ādīnavañca nissaraṇañca yathābhūtaṁ pajānātī”ti. ||| and consciousness.”
Tatiyaṁ.
