Aṅguttara Nikāya 10.128 ||| Numbered Discourses 10.128
13. Parisuddhavagga ||| 13. Purified
Chaṭṭhasutta ||| Sixth
“Dasayime, bhikkhave, dhammā bhāvitā bahulīkatā anuppannā uppajjanti, nāññatra sugatavinayā. ||| “Mendicants, these ten things don’t arise to be developed and cultivated apart from the Holy One’s training.
Katame dasa? ||| What ten?
Sammādiṭṭhi …pe… sammāvimutti— ||| Right view, right purpose, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, right immersion, right knowledge, and right freedom.
ime kho, bhikkhave, dasa dhammā bhāvitā bahulīkatā anuppannā uppajjanti, nāññatra sugatavinayā”ti. ||| These are the ten things that don’t arise to be developed and cultivated apart from the Holy One’s training.”
Chaṭṭhaṁ.
