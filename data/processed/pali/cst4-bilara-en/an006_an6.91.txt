Aṅguttara Nikāya 6.91 ||| Numbered Discourses 6.91
9. Sītivagga ||| 9. Coolness
Abhabbasutta ||| Can’t Give Rise
“Cha, bhikkhave, dhamme abhabbo diṭṭhisampanno puggalo uppādetuṁ. ||| “Mendicants, an individual accomplished in view can’t give rise to six things.
Katame cha? ||| What six?
Sakkāyadiṭṭhiṁ, vicikicchaṁ, sīlabbataparāmāsaṁ, apāyagamanīyaṁ rāgaṁ, apāyagamanīyaṁ dosaṁ, apāyagamanīyaṁ mohaṁ. ||| Substantialist view, doubt, misapprehension of precepts and observances, and forms of greed, hate, and delusion that lead to rebirth in places of loss.
Ime kho, bhikkhave, cha dhamme abhabbo diṭṭhisampanno puggalo uppādetun”ti. ||| An individual accomplished in view can’t give rise to these six things.”
Sattamaṁ.
