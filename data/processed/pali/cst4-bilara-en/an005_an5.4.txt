Aṅguttara Nikāya 5.4 ||| Numbered Discourses 5.4
1. Sekhabalavagga ||| 1. Powers of a Trainee
Yathābhatasutta ||| Cast Down
“Pañcahi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato bhikkhu yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ niraye. ||| “Mendicants, a mendicant with five qualities is placed in hell as if delivered there.
Katamehi pañcahi? ||| What five?
Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu assaddho hoti, ahiriko hoti, anottappī hoti, kusīto hoti, duppañño hoti. ||| It’s when a mendicant is faithless, shameless, imprudent, lazy, and witless.
Imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi dhammehi samannāgato bhikkhu yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ niraye. ||| A mendicant with these five qualities is placed in hell as if delivered there.
Pañcahi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato bhikkhu yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ sagge. ||| A mendicant with five qualities is placed in heaven as if delivered there.
Katamehi pañcahi? ||| What five?
Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu saddho hoti, hirīmā hoti, ottappī hoti, āraddhavīriyo hoti, paññavā hoti. ||| It’s when a mendicant is faithful, conscientious, prudent, energetic, and wise.
Imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi dhammehi samannāgato bhikkhu yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ sagge”ti. ||| A mendicant with these five qualities is placed in heaven as if delivered there.”
Catutthaṁ.
