Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.64 ||| Linked Discourses 1.64
7. Addhavagga ||| 7. Overcome
Saṁyojanasutta ||| Fetter
“Kiṁsu saṁyojano loko, ||| “What fetters the world?
kiṁsu tassa vicāraṇaṁ; ||| How does it travel about?
Kissassu vippahānena, ||| With the giving up of what
nibbānaṁ iti vuccatī”ti. ||| is extinguishment spoken of?”
“Nandīsaṁyojano loko, ||| “Delight fetters the world.
vitakkassa vicāraṇaṁ; ||| It travels about by means of thought.
Taṇhāya vippahānena, ||| With the giving up of craving
nibbānaṁ iti vuccatī”ti. ||| extinguishment is spoken of.”
