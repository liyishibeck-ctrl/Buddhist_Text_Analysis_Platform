Saṁyutta Nikāya 37.11 ||| Linked Discourses 37.11
1. Paṭhamapeyyālavagga ||| 1. Abbreviated Texts
Appassutasutta ||| Unlearned
“Pañcahi, anuruddha, dhammehi samannāgato mātugāmo …pe… nirayaṁ upapajjati.
Katamehi pañcahi?
Assaddho ca hoti, ahiriko ca hoti, anottappī ca hoti, appassuto ca hoti, duppañño ca hoti— ||| “… They’re faithless, shameless, imprudent, unlearned, and witless. …”
imehi kho, anuruddha, pañcahi dhammehi samannāgato mātugāmo …pe… nirayaṁ upapajjatī”ti.
Ekādasamaṁ.
