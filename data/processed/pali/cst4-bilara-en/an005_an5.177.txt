Aṅguttara Nikāya 5.177 ||| Numbered Discourses 5.177
18. Upāsakavagga ||| 18. A Lay Follower
Vaṇijjāsutta ||| Trades
“Pañcimā, bhikkhave, vaṇijjā upāsakena akaraṇīyā. ||| “Mendicants, a lay follower should not engage in these five trades.
Katamā pañca? ||| What five?
Satthavaṇijjā, sattavaṇijjā, maṁsavaṇijjā, majjavaṇijjā, visavaṇijjā— ||| Trade in weapons, living creatures, meat, intoxicants, and poisons.
imā kho, bhikkhave, pañca vaṇijjā upāsakena akaraṇīyā”ti. ||| A lay follower should not engage in these five trades.”
Sattamaṁ.
