Aṅguttara Nikāya 2 ||| Numbered Discourses 2.141–150
13. Dānavagga ||| The Chapter on Giving
141 ||| 141
“Dvemāni, bhikkhave, dānāni. ||| “There are these two gifts.
Katamāni dve? ||| What two?
Āmisadānañca dhammadānañca. ||| A gift of things of the flesh and a gift of the teaching.
Imāni kho, bhikkhave, dve dānāni. ||| These are the two gifts.
Etadaggaṁ, bhikkhave, imesaṁ dvinnaṁ dānānaṁ yadidaṁ dhammadānan”ti. ||| The better of these two gifts is the gift of the teaching.”
