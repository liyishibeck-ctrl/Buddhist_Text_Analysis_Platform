Saṁyutta Nikāya 27.8 ||| Linked Discourses 27.8
1. Kilesavagga ||| 1. Corruptions
Taṇhāsutta ||| Craving
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Yo, bhikkhave, rūpataṇhāya chandarāgo, cittasseso upakkileso. ||| “Mendicants, desire and greed for craving for sights,
Yo saddataṇhāya … ||| craving for sounds,
yo gandhataṇhāya … ||| craving for smells,
yo rasataṇhāya … ||| craving for tastes,
yo phoṭṭhabbataṇhāya … ||| craving for touches,
yo dhammataṇhāya chandarāgo, cittasseso upakkileso. ||| or craving for ideas is a corruption of the mind. …”
Yato kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno …pe…
abhiññā sacchikaraṇīyesu dhammesū”ti.
Aṭṭhamaṁ.
