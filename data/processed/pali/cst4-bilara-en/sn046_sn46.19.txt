Saṁyutta Nikāya 46.19 ||| Linked Discourses 46.19
2. Gilānavagga ||| 2. Sick
Ariyasutta ||| Noble
“Sattime, bhikkhave, bojjhaṅgā bhāvitā bahulīkatā ariyā niyyānikā nīyanti takkarassa sammā dukkhakkhayāya. ||| “Mendicants, when these seven awakening factors are developed and cultivated they are noble and emancipating, and bring one who practices them to the complete ending of suffering.
Katame satta? ||| What seven?
Satisambojjhaṅgo …pe… upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo— ||| The awakening factors of mindfulness, investigation of principles, energy, rapture, tranquility, immersion, and equanimity.
ime kho, bhikkhave, satta bojjhaṅgā bhāvitā bahulīkatā ariyā niyyānikā nīyanti takkarassa sammā dukkhakkhayāyā”ti. ||| When these seven awakening factors are developed and cultivated they are noble and emancipating, and bring one who practices them to the complete ending of suffering.”
Navamaṁ.
