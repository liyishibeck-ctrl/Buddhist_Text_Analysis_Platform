17. Saṭṭhipeyyālavagga ||| 17. Sixty Abbreviated Texts
Ajjhattāyatanadukkhasutta ||| The Interior as Suffering
“Cakkhu, bhikkhave, dukkhaṁ …pe… jivhā dukkhā …pe… mano dukkho. ||| “Mendicants, the eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, and mind are suffering.
Evaṁ passaṁ …pe… ||| Seeing this …
nāparaṁ itthattāyāti pajānātī”ti. ||| They understand: ‘… there is nothing further for this place.’”
