Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.6 ||| Linked Discourses 1.6
1. Naḷavagga ||| 1. A Reed
Jāgarasutta ||| Awake
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
Ekamantaṁ ṭhitā kho sā devatā bhagavato santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi: ||| Standing to one side, that deity spoke this verse in the Buddha’s presence:
“Kati jāgarataṁ suttā, ||| “How many sleep among the waking?
kati suttesu jāgarā; ||| How many wake among the sleeping?
Katibhi rajamādeti, ||| By how many do you gather dust?
katibhi parisujjhatī”ti. ||| By how many are you cleansed?”
“Pañca jāgarataṁ suttā, ||| “Five sleep among the waking.
pañca suttesu jāgarā; ||| Five wake among the sleeping.
Pañcabhi rajamādeti, ||| By five you gather dust.
pañcabhi parisujjhatī”ti. ||| By five you’re cleansed.”
