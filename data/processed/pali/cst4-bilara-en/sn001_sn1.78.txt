Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.78 ||| Linked Discourses 1.78
8. Chetvāvagga ||| 8. Slain
Kāmasutta ||| Desire
“Kimatthakāmo na dade, ||| “What should one who wants what’s best <j>not give away?
kiṁ macco na pariccaje; ||| What should a mortal not forsake?
Kiṁsu muñceyya kalyāṇaṁ, ||| What should be released when it’s good,
pāpikaṁ na ca mocaye”ti. ||| but not when it’s bad?”
“Attānaṁ na dade poso, ||| “A man shouldn’t give away himself.
attānaṁ na pariccaje; ||| He shouldn’t forsake himself.
Vācaṁ muñceyya kalyāṇaṁ, ||| Speech should be released when it’s good,
pāpikañca na mocaye”ti. ||| but not when it’s bad.”
