Aṅguttara Nikāya 7.3 ||| Numbered Discourses 7.3
1. Dhanavagga ||| 1. Wealth
Saṅkhittabalasutta ||| Powers in Brief
Evaṁ me sutaṁ— ||| So I have heard.
ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme …pe… ||| At one time the Buddha was staying near Sāvatthī in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s monastery. …
sattimāni, bhikkhave, balāni. ||| “Mendicants, there are these seven powers.
Katamāni satta? ||| What seven?
Saddhābalaṁ, vīriyabalaṁ, hirībalaṁ, ottappabalaṁ, satibalaṁ, samādhibalaṁ, paññābalaṁ. ||| The powers of faith, energy, conscience, prudence, mindfulness, immersion, and wisdom.
Imāni kho, bhikkhave, satta balānīti. ||| These are the seven powers.
Saddhābalaṁ vīriyañca, ||| The powers are faith and energy,
hirī ottappiyaṁ balaṁ; ||| conscience and prudence,
Satibalaṁ samādhi ca, ||| mindfulness and immersion,
paññā ve sattamaṁ balaṁ; ||| and wisdom as the seventh power.
Etehi balavā bhikkhu, ||| Empowered by these,
sukhaṁ jīvati paṇḍito. ||| an astute mendicant lives happily.
Yoniso vicine dhammaṁ, ||| They should examine the teaching rationally,
paññāyatthaṁ vipassati; ||| discerning the meaning with wisdom.
Pajjotasseva nibbānaṁ, ||| The liberation of their heart
vimokkho hoti cetaso”ti. ||| is like a lamp going out.”
Tatiyaṁ.
