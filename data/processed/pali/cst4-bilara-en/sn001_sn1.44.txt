Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.44 ||| Linked Discourses 1.44
5. Ādittavagga ||| 5. On Fire
Ekamūlasutta ||| One Root
“Ekamūlaṁ dvirāvaṭṭaṁ, ||| “One the root, two the loops,
timalaṁ pañcapattharaṁ; ||| three the stains, five the spreads,
Samuddaṁ dvādasāvaṭṭaṁ, ||| and the ocean, the twelfth whirlpool:
pātālaṁ atarī isī”ti. ||| such is the abyss crossed by the seer.”
