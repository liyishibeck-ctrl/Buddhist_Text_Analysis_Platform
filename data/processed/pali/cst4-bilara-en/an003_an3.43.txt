Aṅguttara Nikāya 3.43 ||| Numbered Discourses 3.43
5. Cūḷavagga ||| 5. The Lesser Chapter
Atthavasasutta ||| Good Reasons
“Tayo, bhikkhave, atthavase sampassamānena alameva paresaṁ dhammaṁ desetuṁ. ||| “Mendicants, taking three reasons into consideration provides quite enough motivation to teach Dhamma to another.
Katame tayo? ||| What three?
Yo dhammaṁ deseti so atthappaṭisaṁvedī ca hoti dhammappaṭisaṁvedī ca. ||| When the teacher understands the meaning and the teaching.
Yo dhammaṁ suṇāti so atthappaṭisaṁvedī ca hoti dhammappaṭisaṁvedī ca. ||| When the audience understands the meaning and the teaching.
Yo ceva dhammaṁ deseti yo ca dhammaṁ suṇāti ubho atthappaṭisaṁvedino ca honti dhammappaṭisaṁvedino ca. ||| When both the teacher and the audience understand the meaning and the teaching.
Ime kho, bhikkhave, tayo atthavase sampassamānena alameva paresaṁ dhammaṁ desetun”ti. ||| Taking these three reasons into consideration provides quite enough motivation to teach Dhamma to another.”
Tatiyaṁ.
