Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.53 ||| Linked Discourses 1.53
6. Jarāvagga ||| 6. Old Age
Mittasutta ||| A Friend
“Kiṁsu pavasato mittaṁ, ||| “Who’s your friend while journeying?
kiṁsu mittaṁ sake ghare; ||| Who’s your friend at home?
Kiṁ mittaṁ atthajātassa, ||| Who’s your friend in need?
kiṁ mittaṁ samparāyikan”ti. ||| Who’s your friend in the next life?”
“Sattho pavasato mittaṁ, ||| “A caravan is your friend while journeying.
mātā mittaṁ sake ghare; ||| Mother is your friend at home.
Sahāyo atthajātassa, ||| An ally in a time of need
hoti mittaṁ punappunaṁ; ||| is a friend time and again.
Sayaṅkatāni puññāni, ||| But the good deeds you’ve done yourself—
taṁ mittaṁ samparāyikan”ti. ||| that’s your friend in the next life.”
