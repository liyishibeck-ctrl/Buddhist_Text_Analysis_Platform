Saṁyutta Nikāya 2.12 ||| Linked Discourses 2.12
2. Anāthapiṇḍikavagga ||| 2. With Anāthapiṇḍika
Veṇḍusutta ||| With Vishnu
Ekamantaṁ ṭhito kho veṇḍu devaputto bhagavato santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi: ||| Standing to one side, the godling Vishnu spoke this verse in the Buddha’s presence:
“Sukhitāva te manujā, ||| “Happy are the men
sugataṁ payirupāsiya; ||| who pay homage to the Holy One!
Yuñjaṁ gotamasāsane, ||| They apply themselves to Gotama’s instructions,
appamattā nu sikkhare”ti. ||| diligently training.”
“Ye me pavutte siṭṭhipade, ||| “Those who practice absorption <j>in accord with the training”,
(veṇḍūti bhagavā) ||| said the Buddha to Vishnu,
Anusikkhanti jhāyino; ||| “in the way of teaching I’ve proclaimed,
Kāle te appamajjantā, ||| diligent while there is still time;
Na maccuvasagā siyun”ti. ||| they won’t fall under the sway of Death.”
