Saṁyutta Nikāya 25.4 ||| Linked Discourses 25.4
1. Cakkhuvagga ||| 1. The Eye
Samphassasutta ||| Contact
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Cakkhusamphasso, bhikkhave, anicco vipariṇāmī aññathābhāvī; ||| “Mendicants, eye contact is impermanent, decaying, and perishing.
sotasamphasso … ||| Ear contact,
ghānasamphasso … ||| nose contact,
jivhāsamphasso … ||| tongue contact,
kāyasamphasso … ||| body contact,
manosamphasso anicco vipariṇāmī aññathābhāvī. ||| and mind contact are impermanent, decaying, and perishing.
Yo, bhikkhave, ime dhamme evaṁ saddahati adhimuccati, ayaṁ vuccati ‘saddhānusārī …pe… ||| Someone who has faith and confidence in these teachings is called a follower by faith. …”
sambodhiparāyano’”ti.
Catutthaṁ.
