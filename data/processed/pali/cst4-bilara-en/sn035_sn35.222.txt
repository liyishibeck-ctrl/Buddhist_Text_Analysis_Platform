17. Saṭṭhipeyyālavagga ||| 17. Sixty Abbreviated Texts
Ajjhattāyatanaaniccasutta ||| The Interior as Impermanent
“Cakkhu, bhikkhave, aniccaṁ …pe… jivhā aniccā …pe… mano anicco. ||| “Mendicants, the eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, and mind are impermanent.
Evaṁ passaṁ …pe… ||| Seeing this …
nāparaṁ itthattāyāti pajānātī”ti. ||| They understand: ‘… there is nothing further for this place.’”
