Aṅguttara Nikāya 3.149 ||| Numbered Discourses 3.149
15. Maṅgalavagga ||| 15. Good Fortune
Asucisutta ||| Impure
“Tīhi, bhikkhave …pe… ||| “Someone with three qualities is placed in hell as if delivered there. …
asucinā kāyakammena, asucinā vacīkammena, asucinā manokammena— ||| Impure deeds by way of body, speech, and mind. …
imehi kho …pe… evaṁ niraye.
Tīhi, bhikkhave …pe… ||| Someone with three qualities is placed in heaven as if delivered there. …
sucinā kāyakammena, sucinā vacīkammena, sucinā manokammena— ||| Pure deeds by way of body, speech, and mind. …”
Imehi kho, bhikkhave, tīhi dhammehi samannāgato yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ sagge”ti.
Catutthaṁ.
