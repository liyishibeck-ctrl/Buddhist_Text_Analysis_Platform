Saṁyutta Nikāya 46.10 ||| Linked Discourses 46.10
1. Pabbatavagga ||| 1. Mountains
Dutiyauppannasutta ||| Arisen (2nd)
“Sattime, bhikkhave, bojjhaṅgā bhāvitā bahulīkatā anuppannā uppajjanti, nāññatra sugatavinayā. ||| “Mendicants, these seven awakening factors don’t arise to be developed and cultivated apart from the Holy One’s training.
Katame satta? ||| What seven?
Satisambojjhaṅgo …pe… upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo— ||| The awakening factors of mindfulness, investigation of principles, energy, rapture, tranquility, immersion, and equanimity.
ime kho, bhikkhave, satta bojjhaṅgā bhāvitā bahulīkatā anuppannā uppajjanti, nāññatra sugatavinayā”ti. ||| These seven awakening factors don’t arise to be developed and cultivated apart from the Holy One’s training.”
Dasamaṁ.
Pabbatavaggo paṭhamo.
Tassuddānaṁ
Himavantaṁ kāyaṁ sīlaṁ,
vatthaṁ bhikkhu ca kuṇḍali;
Kūṭañca upavānañca,
uppannā apare duveti.
