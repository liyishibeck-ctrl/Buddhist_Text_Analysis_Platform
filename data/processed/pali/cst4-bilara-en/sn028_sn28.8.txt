Saṁyutta Nikāya 28.8 ||| Linked Discourses 28.8
1. Sāriputtavagga ||| 1. With Sāriputta
Nevasaññānāsaññāyatanasutta ||| The Dimension of Neither Perception Nor Non-Perception
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
Atha kho āyasmā sāriputto …pe… ||| Venerable Ānanda saw Venerable Sāriputta …
“idhāhaṁ, āvuso, ākiñcaññāyatanaṁ samatikkamma nevasaññānāsaññāyatanaṁ upasampajja viharāmi …pe… ||| “Reverend, going totally beyond the dimension of nothingness, I entered and remained in the dimension of neither perception nor non-perception. …” …
vuṭṭhitoti vā”ti.
Aṭṭhamaṁ.
