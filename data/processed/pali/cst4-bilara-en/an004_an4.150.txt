Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.150 ||| Numbered Discourses 4.150
15. Ābhāvagga ||| 15. Brightness
Sārasutta ||| Essentials
“Cattārome, bhikkhave, sārā. ||| “Mendicants, there are these four essentials.
Katame cattāro? ||| What four?
Sīlasāro, samādhisāro, paññāsāro, vimuttisāro— ||| Ethics, immersion, wisdom, and freedom are essentials.
Ime kho, bhikkhave, cattāro sārā”ti. ||| These are the four essentials.”
Dasamaṁ.
Ābhāvaggo pañcamo.
Tassuddānaṁ
Ābhā pabhā ca ālokā,
obhāsā ceva pajjotā;
Dve kālā caritā dve ca,
honti sārena te dasāti.
Tatiyo paṇṇāsako samatto.
