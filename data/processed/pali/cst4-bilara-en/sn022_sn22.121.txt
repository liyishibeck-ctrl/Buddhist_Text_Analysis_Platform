Saṁyutta Nikāya 22.121 ||| Linked Discourses 22.121
12. Dhammakathikavagga ||| 12. A Dhamma speaker
Upādāniyasutta ||| Things That Fuel Grasping
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Upādāniye ca, bhikkhave, dhamme desessāmi upādānañca. ||| “Mendicants, I will teach you the things that fuel grasping, and the grasping.
Taṁ suṇātha. ||| Listen …
Katame ca, bhikkhave, upādāniyā dhammā, katamaṁ upādānaṁ? ||| What are the things that fuel grasping? And what is the grasping?
Rūpaṁ, bhikkhave, upādāniyo dhammo, ||| Form is something that fuels grasping.
yo tattha chandarāgo, taṁ tattha upādānaṁ. ||| The desire and greed for it is the grasping.
Vedanā …pe… ||| Feeling …
saññā … ||| Perception …
saṅkhārā … ||| Choices …
viññāṇaṁ upādāniyo dhammo; ||| Consciousness is something that fuels grasping.
yo tattha chandarāgo, taṁ tattha upādānaṁ. ||| The desire and greed for it is the grasping.
Ime vuccanti, bhikkhave, upādāniyā dhammā, idaṁ upādānan”ti. ||| These are called the things that fuel grasping, and this is the grasping.”
Navamaṁ.
