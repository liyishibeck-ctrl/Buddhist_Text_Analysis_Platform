Saṁyutta Nikāya 32.1 ||| Linked Discourses 32.1
1. Valāhakavagga ||| 1. Gods of the Thunderclouds
Suddhikasutta ||| Plain Version
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Valāhakakāyike vo, bhikkhave, deve desessāmi. ||| “Mendicants, I will teach you about the gods of the thundercloud host.
Taṁ suṇātha. ||| Listen …
Katame ca, bhikkhave, valāhakakāyikā devā? ||| And what are the gods of the thundercloud host?
Santi, bhikkhave, sītavalāhakā devā; ||| There are gods of the cool thunderclouds,
santi uṇhavalāhakā devā; ||| warm thunderclouds,
santi abbhavalāhakā devā; ||| stormy thunderclouds,
santi vātavalāhakā devā; ||| windy thunderclouds,
santi vassavalāhakā devā— ||| and rainy thunderclouds.
ime vuccanti, bhikkhave, ‘valāhakakāyikā devā’”ti. ||| These are called gods of the thundercloud host.”
Paṭhamaṁ.
