Saṁyutta Nikāya 46.22 ||| Linked Discourses 46.22
3. Udāyivagga ||| 3. With Udāyī
Bojjhaṅgadesanāsutta ||| A Teaching on the Awakening Factors
“Satta vo, bhikkhave, bojjhaṅge desessāmi; ||| “Mendicants, I will teach you the seven awakening factors.
taṁ suṇātha. ||| Listen …
Katame ca, bhikkhave, satta bojjhaṅgā? ||| And what are the seven awakening factors?
Satisambojjhaṅgo …pe… upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo— ||| The awakening factors of mindfulness, investigation of principles, energy, rapture, tranquility, immersion, and equanimity.
ime kho, bhikkhave, satta bojjhaṅgā”ti. ||| These are the seven awakening factors.”
Dutiyaṁ.
