Saṁyutta Nikāya 2.2 ||| Linked Discourses 2.2
1. Paṭhamavagga ||| Chapter One
Dutiyakassapasutta ||| With Kassapa (2nd)
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
Ekamantaṁ ṭhito kho kassapo devaputto bhagavato santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi: ||| Standing to one side, the godling Kassapa spoke this verse in the Buddha’s presence:
“Bhikkhu siyā jhāyī vimuttacitto, ||| “If a mendicant is a meditator, freed in mind,
Ākaṅkhe ce hadayassānupattiṁ; ||| and if they desire the heart’s fulfillment—
Lokassa ñatvā udayabbayañca, ||| having known the arising and passing of the world,
Sucetaso anissito tadānisaṁso”ti. ||| good-hearted, independent, that is their reward.”
