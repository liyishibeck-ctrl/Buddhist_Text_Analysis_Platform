Saṁyutta Nikāya 9.12 ||| Linked Discourses 9.12
1. Vanavagga ||| 1. In the Woods
Majjhanhikasutta ||| Midday
Ekaṁ samayaṁ aññataro bhikkhu kosalesu viharati aññatarasmiṁ vanasaṇḍe. ||| At one time one of the mendicants was staying in the land of the Kosalans in a certain forest grove.
Atha kho tasmiṁ vanasaṇḍe adhivatthā devatā yena so bhikkhu tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā tassa bhikkhuno santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi: ||| The deity haunting that forest approached that mendicant and spoke this verse in their presence:
“Ṭhite majjhanhike kāle, ||| “In the still of high noon,
sannisīvesu pakkhisu; ||| when the birds have settled down,
Saṇateva brahāraññaṁ, ||| the mighty forest itself resounds—
taṁ bhayaṁ paṭibhāti maṁ. ||| that strikes me as so scary!”
Ṭhite majjhanhike kāle, ||| “In the still of high noon,
sannisīvesu pakkhisu; ||| when the birds have settled down,
Saṇateva brahāraññaṁ, ||| the mighty forest itself resounds—
sā rati paṭibhāti man”ti. ||| that strikes me as so delightful!”
