13. Oghavagga ||| 13. Floods
Oghādisutta ||| Floods
“Cattārome, bhikkhave, oghā. ||| “Mendicants, there are these four floods.
Katame cattāro? ||| What four?
Kāmogho, bhavogho, diṭṭhogho, avijjogho”ti. ||| The floods of sensuality, desire to be reborn, views, and ignorance.”
vitthāretabbaṁ. ||| (Tell in full as in SN 45.171–179.)
