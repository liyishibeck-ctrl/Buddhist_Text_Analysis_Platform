Saṁyutta Nikāya 45.172 ||| Linked Discourses 45.172
14. Oghavagga ||| 14. Floods
Yogasutta ||| Yokes
“Cattārome, bhikkhave, yogā. ||| “Mendicants, there are these four yokes.
Katame cattāro? ||| What four?
Kāmayogo, bhavayogo, diṭṭhiyogo avijjāyogo— ||| The yokes of sensual pleasures, future lives, views, and ignorance.
ime kho, bhikkhave, cattāro yogā. ||| These are the four yokes.
Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, catunnaṁ yogānaṁ abhiññāya pariññāya parikkhayāya pahānāya …pe… ayaṁ ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo bhāvetabbo”ti. ||| The noble eightfold path should be developed for the direct knowledge, complete understanding, finishing, and giving up of these four yokes.”
Dutiyaṁ.
