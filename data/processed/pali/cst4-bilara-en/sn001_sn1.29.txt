Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.29 ||| Linked Discourses 1.29
3. Sattivagga ||| 3. A Sword
Catucakkasutta ||| Four Wheels
“Catucakkaṁ navadvāraṁ, ||| “Four are its wheels, and nine its doors;
puṇṇaṁ lobhena saṁyutaṁ; ||| it’s stuffed full, bound with greed,
Paṅkajātaṁ mahāvīra, ||| and born from a bog.
kathaṁ yātrā bhavissatī”ti. ||| Great hero, how will I keep going?”
“Chetvā naddhiṁ varattañca, ||| “Having cut the strap and harness—
icchā lobhañca pāpakaṁ; ||| the wicked desire and greed—
Samūlaṁ taṇhamabbuyha, ||| and having plucked out craving, root and all:
evaṁ yātrā bhavissatī”ti. ||| that’s how you will keep going.”
