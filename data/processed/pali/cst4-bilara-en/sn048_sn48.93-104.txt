10. Balakaraṇīyavagga ||| 10. The Chapter on Hard Work
Balakaraṇīyavagga ||| The Chapter on Hard Work
(Balakaraṇīyavaggo vitthāretabbo.) ||| (Tell in full as in the chapter on hard work at SN 45.149–160.)
Tassuddānaṁ
Balaṁ bījañca nāgo ca, ||| Hard work, seeds, and dragons,
rukkho kumbhena sūkiyā; ||| a tree, a pot, and a spike,
Ākāsena ca dve meghā, ||| the atmosphere, and two on clouds,
nāvā āgantukā nadīti. ||| a ship, a guest house, and a river.
