Saṁyutta Nikāya 11.8 ||| Linked Discourses 11.8
1. Paṭhamavagga ||| Chapter One
Verocanaasurindasutta ||| Verocana, Lord of Titans
Sāvatthiyaṁ jetavane. ||| Near Sāvatthī in Jeta’s Grove.
Tena kho pana samayena bhagavā divāvihāragato hoti paṭisallīno. ||| Now at that time the Buddha had gone into retreat for the day’s meditation.
Atha kho sakko ca devānamindo verocano ca asurindo yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkamiṁsu; upasaṅkamitvā paccekaṁ dvārabāhaṁ nissāya aṭṭhaṁsu. ||| Then Sakka, lord of gods, and Verocana, lord of titans, approached the Buddha and stationed themselves one by each door-post.
Atha kho verocano asurindo bhagavato santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi: ||| Then Verocana spoke this verse in the Buddha’s presence:
“Vāyametheva puriso, ||| “A man should make an effort
yāva atthassa nipphadā; ||| until his goal is accomplished.
Nipphannasobhano attho, ||| A goal is splendid when accomplished—
verocanavaco idan”ti. ||| this is the word of Verocana!”
“Vāyametheva puriso, ||| “A man should make an effort
yāva atthassa nipphadā; ||| until his goal is accomplished.
Nipphannasobhano attho, ||| For the goal that’s splendid when accomplished,
khantyā bhiyyo na vijjatī”ti. ||| naught better than patience is found.”
“Sabbe sattā atthajātā, ||| “All beings are in need,
tattha tattha yathārahaṁ; ||| as they deserve in each case.
Saṁyogaparamā tveva, ||| But even so, harnessed work is the highest
sambhogā sabbapāṇinaṁ; ||| thing of value for all living creatures.
Nipphannasobhano attho, ||| A goal is splendid when accomplished—
verocanavaco idan”ti. ||| this is the word of Verocana!”
“Sabbe sattā atthajātā, ||| “All beings are in need,
tattha tattha yathārahaṁ; ||| as they deserve in each case.
Saṁyogaparamā tveva, ||| But rather than harnessed work being the highest
sambhogā sabbapāṇinaṁ; ||| thing of value for all living creatures,
Nipphannasobhano attho, ||| for the goal that’s splendid when accomplished,
khantyā bhiyyo na vijjatī”ti. ||| naught better than patience is found.”
