Saṁyutta Nikāya 23.18 ||| Linked Discourses 23.18
2. Dutiyamāravagga ||| 2. About Māra (2nd)
Anattadhammasutta ||| Naturally Not-Self
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho āyasmā rādho bhagavantaṁ etadavoca: ||| Seated to one side, Venerable Rādha said to the Buddha:
“‘anattadhammo, anattadhammo’ti, bhante, vuccati. ||| “Sir, they speak of having ‘not-self nature’.
Katamo nu kho, bhante, anattadhammo”ti? ||| What has a nature of not-self?”
“Rūpaṁ kho, rādha, anattadhammo, vedanā anattadhammo, saññā anattadhammo, saṅkhārā anattadhammo, viññāṇaṁ anattadhammo. ||| “Rādha, form, feeling, perception, choices, and consciousness have a nature of not-self.
Evaṁ passaṁ …pe… ||| Seeing this …
nāparaṁ itthattāyāti pajānātī”ti. ||| They understand: ‘… there is nothing further for this place.’”
Aṭṭhamaṁ.
