Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.17 ||| Linked Discourses 1.17
2. Nandanavagga ||| 2. The Garden of Delight
Dukkarasutta ||| Hard to Do
“Dukkaraṁ duttitikkhañca, ||| “Hard to do, hard to endure,
abyattena ca sāmaññaṁ; ||| is the ascetic life for the inept,
Bahūhi tattha sambādhā, ||| for it has many narrow passes
yattha bālo visīdatī”ti. ||| where the fool founders.”
“Katihaṁ careyya sāmaññaṁ, ||| “How many days could an ascetic live
cittañce na nivāraye; ||| without controlling the mind?
Pade pade visīdeyya, ||| They’d founder with each step,
saṅkappānaṁ vasānugoti. ||| under the sway of thoughts.
Kummova aṅgāni sake kapāle, ||| A mendicant should collect their thoughts
Samodahaṁ bhikkhu manovitakke; ||| as a tortoise draws its limbs into its shell.
Anissito aññamaheṭhayāno, ||| Independent, not disturbing others,
Parinibbuto nūpavadeyya kañcī”ti. ||| quenched: they wouldn’t blame anyone.”
