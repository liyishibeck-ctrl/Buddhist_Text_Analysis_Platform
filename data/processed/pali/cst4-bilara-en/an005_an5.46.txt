Aṅguttara Nikāya 5.46 ||| Numbered Discourses 5.46
5. Muṇḍarājavagga ||| 5. With King Muṇḍa
Sampadāsutta ||| Success
“Pañcimā, bhikkhave, sampadā. ||| “Mendicants, there are five accomplishments.
Katamā pañca? ||| What five?
Saddhāsampadā, sīlasampadā, sutasampadā, cāgasampadā, paññāsampadā— ||| Accomplishment in faith, ethics, learning, generosity, and wisdom.
imā kho, bhikkhave, pañca sampadā”ti. ||| These are the five accomplishments.”
Chaṭṭhaṁ.
