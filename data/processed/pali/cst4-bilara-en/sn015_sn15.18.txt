Saṁyutta Nikāya 15.18 ||| Linked Discourses 15.18
2. Dutiyavagga ||| Chapter Two
Puttasutta ||| Son
Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati …pe… ||| At Sāvatthī.
“na so, bhikkhave, satto sulabharūpo yo naputtabhūtapubbo …pe… ||| “It’s not easy to find a sentient being who in all this long time has not previously been your son. …
alaṁ vimuccitun”ti. ||| This is quite enough for you to become disillusioned, dispassionate, and freed regarding all conditions.”
Aṭṭhamaṁ.
