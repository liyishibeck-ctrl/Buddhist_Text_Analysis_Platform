Aṅguttara Nikāya 10.1 ||| Numbered Discourses 10.1
1. Ānisaṁsavagga ||| 1. Benefits
Kimatthiyasutta ||| What’s the Goal?
Evaṁ me sutaṁ— ||| So I have heard.
ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. ||| At one time the Buddha was staying near Sāvatthī in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s monastery.
Atha kho āyasmā ānando yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi. Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho āyasmā ānando bhagavantaṁ etadavoca: ||| Then Venerable Ānanda went up to the Buddha, bowed, sat down to one side, and said to him:
“Kimatthiyāni, bhante, kusalāni sīlāni kimānisaṁsānī”ti? ||| “Sir, what is the goal and benefit of skillful ethics?”
“Avippaṭisāratthāni kho, ānanda, kusalāni sīlāni avippaṭisārānisaṁsānī”ti. ||| “Ānanda, having no regrets is the goal and benefit of skillful ethics.”
“Avippaṭisāro pana, bhante, kimatthiyo kimānisaṁso”ti? ||| “But what is the goal and benefit of having no regrets?”
“Avippaṭisāro kho, ānanda, pāmojjattho pāmojjānisaṁso”ti. ||| “Joy is the goal and benefit of having no regrets.”
“Pāmojjaṁ pana, bhante, kimatthiyaṁ kimānisaṁsan”ti? ||| “But what is the goal and benefit of joy?”
“Pāmojjaṁ kho, ānanda, pītatthaṁ pītānisaṁsan”ti. ||| “Rapture …”
“Pīti pana, bhante, kimatthiyā kimānisaṁsā”ti? ||| “But what is the goal and benefit of rapture?”
“Pīti kho, ānanda, passaddhatthā passaddhānisaṁsā”ti. ||| “Tranquility …”
“Passaddhi pana, bhante, kimatthiyā kimānisaṁsā”ti? ||| “But what is the goal and benefit of tranquility?”
“Passaddhi kho, ānanda, sukhatthā sukhānisaṁsā”ti. ||| “Bliss …”
“Sukhaṁ pana, bhante, kimatthiyaṁ kimānisaṁsan”ti? ||| “But what is the goal and benefit of bliss?”
“Sukhaṁ kho, ānanda, samādhatthaṁ samādhānisaṁsan”ti. ||| “Immersion …”
“Samādhi pana, bhante, kimatthiyo kimānisaṁso”ti? ||| “But what is the goal and benefit of immersion?”
“Samādhi kho, ānanda, yathābhūtañāṇadassanattho yathābhūtañāṇadassanānisaṁso”ti. ||| “Truly knowing and seeing …”
“Yathābhūtañāṇadassanaṁ pana, bhante, kimatthiyaṁ kimānisaṁsan”ti? ||| “But what is the goal and benefit of truly knowing and seeing?”
“Yathābhūtañāṇadassanaṁ kho, ānanda, nibbidāvirāgatthaṁ nibbidāvirāgānisaṁsan”ti. ||| “Disillusionment and dispassion …”
“Nibbidāvirāgo pana, bhante, kimatthiyo kimānisaṁso”ti? ||| “But what is the goal and benefit of disillusionment and dispassion?”
“Nibbidāvirāgo kho, ānanda, vimuttiñāṇadassanattho vimuttiñāṇadassanānisaṁso. ||| “Knowledge and vision of freedom is the goal and benefit of disillusionment and dispassion.
Iti kho, ānanda, kusalāni sīlāni avippaṭisāratthāni avippaṭisārānisaṁsāni; ||| So, Ānanda, the goal and benefit of skillful ethics is not having regrets.
avippaṭisāro pāmojjattho pāmojjānisaṁso; ||| Joy is the goal and benefit of not having regrets.
pāmojjaṁ pītatthaṁ pītānisaṁsaṁ; ||| Rapture is the goal and benefit of joy.
pīti passaddhatthā passaddhānisaṁsā; ||| Tranquility is the goal and benefit of rapture.
passaddhi sukhatthā sukhānisaṁsā; ||| Bliss is the goal and benefit of tranquility.
sukhaṁ samādhatthaṁ samādhānisaṁsaṁ; ||| Immersion is the goal and benefit of bliss.
samādhi yathābhūtañāṇadassanattho yathābhūtañāṇadassanānisaṁso; ||| Truly knowing and seeing is the goal and benefit of immersion.
yathābhūtañāṇadassanaṁ nibbidāvirāgatthaṁ nibbidāvirāgānisaṁsaṁ; ||| Disillusionment and dispassion is the goal and benefit of truly knowing and seeing.
nibbidāvirāgo vimuttiñāṇadassanattho vimuttiñāṇadassanānisaṁso. ||| Knowledge and vision of freedom is the goal and benefit of disillusionment and dispassion.
Iti kho, ānanda, kusalāni sīlāni anupubbena aggāya parentī”ti. ||| So, Ānanda, skillful ethics progressively lead up to the highest.”
Paṭhamaṁ.
