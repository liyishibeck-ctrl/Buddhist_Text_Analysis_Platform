Aṅguttara Nikāya 3.138 ||| Numbered Discourses 3.138
14. Yodhājīvavagga ||| 14. A Warrior
Sampadāsutta ||| Accomplishment
“Tisso imā, bhikkhave, sampadā. ||| “Mendicants, there are three accomplishments.
Katamā tisso? ||| What three?
Saddhāsampadā, sīlasampadā, paññāsampadā— ||| Accomplishment in faith, ethics, and wisdom.
imā kho, bhikkhave, tisso sampadā”ti. ||| These are the three accomplishments.”
Chaṭṭhaṁ.
