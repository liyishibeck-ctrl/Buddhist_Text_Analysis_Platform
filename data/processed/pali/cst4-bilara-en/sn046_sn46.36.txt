Saṁyutta Nikāya 46.36 ||| Linked Discourses 46.36
4. Nīvaraṇavagga ||| 4. Hindrances
Buddhisutta ||| Growth
“Sattime, bhikkhave, bojjhaṅgā bhāvitā bahulīkatā buddhiyā aparihānāya saṁvattanti. ||| “Mendicants, when the seven awakening factors are developed and cultivated they lead to growth and progress.
Katame satta? ||| What seven?
Satisambojjhaṅgo …pe… upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo. ||| The awakening factors of mindfulness, investigation of principles, energy, rapture, tranquility, immersion, and equanimity.
Ime kho, bhikkhave, satta bojjhaṅgā bhāvitā bahulīkatā buddhiyā aparihānāya saṁvattantī”ti. ||| When these seven awakening factors are developed and cultivated they lead to growth and progress.”
Sattamaṁ.
