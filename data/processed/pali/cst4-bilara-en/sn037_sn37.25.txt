Saṁyutta Nikāya 37.25 ||| Linked Discourses 37.25
3. Balavagga ||| 3. Powers
Visāradasutta ||| Assured
“Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, mātugāmassa balāni. ||| “Mendicants, there are these five powers of a lady.
Katamāni pañca? ||| What five?
Rūpabalaṁ, bhogabalaṁ, ñātibalaṁ, puttabalaṁ, sīlabalaṁ— ||| Attractiveness, wealth, relatives, children, and ethical behavior.
imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañca mātugāmassa balāni. ||| These are the five powers of a lady.
Imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi balehi samannāgato mātugāmo visārado agāraṁ ajjhāvasatī”ti. ||| A lady living at home with these five qualities is self-assured.”
Paṭhamaṁ.
