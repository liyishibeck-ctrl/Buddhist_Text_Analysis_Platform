Saṁyutta Nikāya 17.6 ||| Linked Discourses 17.6
1. Paṭhamavagga ||| Chapter One
Asanisutta ||| A Bolt of Lightning
Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Dāruṇo, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko …pe… adhigamāya. ||| “Possessions, honor, and popularity are grim …
Kaṁ, bhikkhave, asanivicakkaṁ āgacchatu, sekhaṁ appattamānasaṁ lābhasakkārasiloko anupāpuṇātu. ||| Let forked lightning fall on whom? Let possessions, honor, and popularity come to a trainee who has not achieved their heart’s desire.
Asanivicakkanti kho, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasilokassetaṁ adhivacanaṁ. ||| ‘Forked lightning’ is a term for possessions, honor, and popularity.
Evaṁ dāruṇo kho, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko …pe… ||| So grim are possessions, honor, and popularity. …”
evañhi vo, bhikkhave, sikkhitabban”ti.
Chaṭṭhaṁ.
