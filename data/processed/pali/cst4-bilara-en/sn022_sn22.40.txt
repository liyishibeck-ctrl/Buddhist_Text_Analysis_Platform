Saṁyutta Nikāya 22.40 ||| Linked Discourses 22.40
4. Natumhākavagga ||| 4. It’s Not Yours
Dutiyaanudhammasutta ||| In Line With the Teaching (2nd)
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Dhammānudhammappaṭipannassa, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno ayamanudhammo hoti yaṁ rūpe aniccānupassī vihareyya …pe… ||| “Mendicants, when a mendicant is practicing in line with the teaching, this is what’s in line with the teaching. They should meditate observing impermanence in form, feeling, perception, choices, and consciousness. …
‘parimuccati dukkhasmā’ti vadāmī”ti. ||| They’re freed from suffering, I say.”
Aṭṭhamaṁ.
