Saṁyutta Nikāya 25.9 ||| Linked Discourses 25.9
1. Cakkhuvagga ||| 1. The Eye
Pathavīdhātusutta ||| Elements
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Pathavīdhātu, bhikkhave, aniccā vipariṇāmī aññathābhāvī; ||| “Mendicants, the earth element is impermanent, decaying, and perishing.
āpodhātu … ||| The water element,
tejodhātu … ||| the fire element,
vāyodhātu … ||| the air element,
ākāsadhātu … ||| the space element,
viññāṇadhātu aniccā vipariṇāmī aññathābhāvī. ||| and the consciousness element are impermanent, decaying, and perishing.
Yo, bhikkhave, ime dhamme evaṁ saddahati adhimuccati, ayaṁ vuccati ‘saddhānusārī …pe… ||| Someone who has faith and confidence in these teachings is called a follower by faith. …”
sambodhiparāyano’”ti.
Navamaṁ.
