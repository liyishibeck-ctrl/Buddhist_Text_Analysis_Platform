12. Dhammakathikavagga ||| 12. A Dhamma speaker
Saṁyojaniyasutta ||| Things That Tighten Fetters
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Saṁyojaniye ca, bhikkhave, dhamme desessāmi saṁyojanañca. ||| “Mendicants, I will teach you the things that tighten the fetters, and the fetter.
Taṁ suṇātha. ||| Listen …
Katame ca, bhikkhave, saṁyojaniyā dhammā, katamaṁ saṁyojanaṁ? ||| What are the things that tighten the fetters? And what is the fetter?
Rūpaṁ, bhikkhave, saṁyojaniyo dhammo; ||| Form is something that tightens the fetters.
yo tattha chandarāgo, taṁ tattha saṁyojanaṁ. ||| The desire and greed for it is the fetter.
Vedanā …pe… ||| Feeling …
saññā … ||| Perception …
saṅkhārā … ||| Choices …
viññāṇaṁ saṁyojaniyo dhammo; ||| Consciousness is something that tightens the fetters.
yo tattha chandarāgo, taṁ tattha saṁyojanaṁ. ||| The desire and greed for it is the fetter.
Ime vuccanti, bhikkhave, saṁyojaniyā dhammā, idaṁ saṁyojanan”ti. ||| These are called the things that tighten the fetters, and this is the fetter.”
Aṭṭhamaṁ.
