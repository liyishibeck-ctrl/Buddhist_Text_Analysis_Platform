Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.7 ||| Linked Discourses 1.7
1. Naḷavagga ||| 1. A Reed
Appaṭividitasutta ||| Not Comprehending
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
Ekamantaṁ ṭhitā kho sā devatā bhagavato santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi: ||| Standing to one side, that deity spoke this verse in the Buddha’s presence:
“Yesaṁ dhammā appaṭividitā, ||| “Those have not deciphered the teachings
Paravādesu nīyare; ||| may be led astray by the doctrines of others.
Suttā te nappabujjhanti, ||| Asleep, they have not woken up:
Kālo tesaṁ pabujjhitun”ti. ||| it is time for them to wake!”
“Yesaṁ dhammā suppaṭividitā, ||| “Those who have well deciphered the teachings
Paravādesu na nīyare; ||| won’t be led astray by the doctrines of others.
Te sambuddhā sammadaññā, ||| They have woken up, they rightly know,
Caranti visame saman”ti. ||| smoothly they walk in the rough.”
