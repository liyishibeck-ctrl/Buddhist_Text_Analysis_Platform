Aṅguttara Nikāya 8.67 ||| Numbered Discourses 8.67
7. Bhūmicālavagga ||| 7. Earthquakes
Anariyavohārasutta ||| Ignoble Expressions
“Aṭṭhime, bhikkhave, anariyavohārā. ||| “Mendicants, there are these eight ignoble expressions.
Katame aṭṭha? ||| What eight?
Adiṭṭhe diṭṭhavāditā, asute sutavāditā, amute mutavāditā, aviññāte viññātavāditā, diṭṭhe adiṭṭhavāditā, sute asutavāditā, mute amutavāditā, viññāte aviññātavāditā. ||| Saying you’ve seen, heard, thought, or known something, but you haven’t. And saying you haven’t seen, heard, thought, or known something, and you have.
Ime kho, bhikkhave, aṭṭha anariyavohārā”ti. ||| These are the eight ignoble expressions.”
Sattamaṁ.
