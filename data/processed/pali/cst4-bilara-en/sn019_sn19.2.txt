Saṁyutta Nikāya 19.2 ||| Linked Discourses 19.2
1. Paṭhamavagga ||| Chapter One
Pesisutta ||| A Piece of Meat
“Idhāhaṁ, āvuso, gijjhakūṭā pabbatā orohanto addasaṁ maṁsapesiṁ vehāsaṁ gacchantiṁ. ||| “Just now, reverend, as I was descending from Vulture’s Peak Mountain I saw a scrap of meat flying through the air.
Tamenaṁ gijjhāpi kākāpi kulalāpi anupatitvā anupatitvā vitacchenti virājenti. ||| Vultures, crows, and hawks kept chasing it, pecking and clawing
Sā sudaṁ aṭṭassaraṁ karoti …pe… ||| as it screeched in pain. …” …
eso, bhikkhave, satto imasmiṁyeva rājagahe goghātako ahosi …pe…. ||| “That being used to be a cattle butcher right here in Rājagaha. …”
Dutiyaṁ.
