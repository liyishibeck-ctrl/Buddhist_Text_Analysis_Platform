Saṁyutta Nikāya 23.13 ||| Linked Discourses 23.13
2. Dutiyamāravagga ||| 2. About Māra (2nd)
Aniccasutta ||| Impermanence
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho āyasmā rādho bhagavantaṁ etadavoca: ||| Seated to one side, Venerable Rādha said to the Buddha:
“‘aniccaṁ, aniccan’ti, bhante, vuccati. ||| “Sir, they speak of ‘impermanence’.
Katamaṁ nu kho, bhante, aniccan”ti? ||| What has impermanent nature?”
“Rūpaṁ kho, rādha, aniccaṁ, vedanā aniccā, saññā aniccā, saṅkhārā aniccā, viññāṇaṁ aniccaṁ. ||| “Rādha, form, feeling, perception, choices, and consciousness are impermanent.
Evaṁ passaṁ …pe… ||| Seeing this …
nāparaṁ itthattāyāti pajānātī”ti. ||| They understand: ‘… there is nothing further for this place.’”
Tatiyaṁ.
