Saṁyutta Nikāya 25.3 ||| Linked Discourses 25.3
1. Cakkhuvagga ||| 1. The Eye
Viññāṇasutta ||| Consciousness
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Cakkhuviññāṇaṁ, bhikkhave, aniccaṁ vipariṇāmi aññathābhāvi; ||| “Mendicants, eye consciousness is impermanent, decaying, and perishing.
sotaviññāṇaṁ … ||| Ear consciousness,
ghānaviññāṇaṁ … ||| nose consciousness,
jivhāviññāṇaṁ … ||| tongue consciousness,
kāyaviññāṇaṁ … ||| body consciousness,
manoviññāṇaṁ aniccaṁ vipariṇāmi aññathābhāvi. ||| and mind consciousness are impermanent, decaying, and perishing.
Yo bhikkhave …pe… ||| Someone who has faith and confidence in these teachings is called a follower by faith. …”
sambodhiparāyano”ti.
Tatiyaṁ.
