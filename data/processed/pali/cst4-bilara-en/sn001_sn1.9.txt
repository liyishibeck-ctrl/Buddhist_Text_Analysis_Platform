Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.9 ||| Linked Discourses 1.9
1. Naḷavagga ||| 1. A Reed
Mānakāmasutta ||| Fond of Conceit
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
Ekamantaṁ ṭhitā kho sā devatā bhagavato santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi: ||| Standing to one side, that deity spoke this verse in the Buddha’s presence:
“Na mānakāmassa damo idhatthi, ||| “One fond of conceit can’t be tamed,
Na monamatthi asamāhitassa; ||| no sage is lacking immersion.
Eko araññe viharaṁ pamatto, ||| Living negligent alone in the wilderness,
Na maccudheyyassa tareyya pāran”ti. ||| they can’t cross beyond Death’s dominion.”
“Mānaṁ pahāya susamāhitatto, ||| “Having given up conceit, serene within oneself,
Sucetaso sabbadhi vippamutto; ||| with a good heart, everywhere free;
Eko araññe viharaṁ appamatto, ||| living diligent alone in the wilderness,
Sa maccudheyyassa tareyya pāran”ti. ||| they cross beyond Death’s dominion.”
