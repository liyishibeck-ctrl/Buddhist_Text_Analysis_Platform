Saṁyutta Nikāya 45.15 ||| Linked Discourses 45.15
2. Vihāravagga ||| 2. Meditation
Dutiyauppādasutta ||| Arising (2nd)
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Aṭṭhime, bhikkhave, dhammā bhāvitā bahulīkatā anuppannā uppajjanti, nāññatra sugatavinayā. ||| “Mendicants, these eight things don’t arise to be developed and cultivated apart from the Holy One’s training.
Katame aṭṭha? ||| What eight?
Seyyathidaṁ—sammādiṭṭhi …pe… sammāsamādhi. ||| They are: right view, right purpose, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, and right immersion.
Ime kho, bhikkhave, aṭṭha dhammā bhāvitā bahulīkatā anuppannā uppajjanti, nāññatra sugatavinayā”ti. ||| These are the eight things that don’t arise to be developed and cultivated apart from the Holy One’s training.”
Pañcamaṁ.
