Aṅguttara Nikāya 5.125 ||| Numbered Discourses 5.125
13. Gilānavagga ||| 13. Sick
Paṭhamaanāyussāsutta ||| Longevity (1st)
“Pañcime, bhikkhave, dhammā anāyussā. ||| “Mendicants, these five things impede longevity.
Katame pañca? ||| What five?
Asappāyakārī hoti, sappāye mattaṁ na jānāti, apariṇatabhojī ca hoti, akālacārī ca hoti, abrahmacārī ca. ||| Doing what is unsuitable, not knowing moderation in what is suitable, eating food unfit for consumption, activity at unsuitable times, and unchastity.
Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca dhammā anāyussā. ||| These are the five things that impede longevity.
Pañcime, bhikkhave, dhammā āyussā. ||| These five things promote longevity.
Katame pañca? ||| What five?
Sappāyakārī hoti, sappāye mattaṁ jānāti, pariṇatabhojī ca hoti, kālacārī ca hoti, brahmacārī ca. ||| Doing what is suitable, knowing moderation in what is suitable, eating food fit for consumption, activity at suitable times, and chastity.
Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca dhammā āyussā”ti. ||| These are the five things that promote longevity.”
Pañcamaṁ.
