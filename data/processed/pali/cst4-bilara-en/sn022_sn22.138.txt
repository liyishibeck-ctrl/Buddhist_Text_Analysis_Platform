Saṁyutta Nikāya 22.138 ||| Linked Discourses 22.138
14. Kukkuḷavagga ||| 14. Burning Chaff
Dutiyaaniccasutta ||| Impermanence (2nd)
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Yaṁ, bhikkhave, aniccaṁ; tatra vo rāgo pahātabbo. ||| “Mendicants, you should give up greed for what is impermanent.
Kiñca, bhikkhave, aniccaṁ? ||| And what is impermanent?
Rūpaṁ, bhikkhave, aniccaṁ; tatra vo rāgo pahātabbo. ||| Form is impermanent; you should give up greed for it.
Vedanā aniccā … ||| Feeling …
saññā … ||| Perception …
saṅkhārā … ||| Choices …
viññāṇaṁ aniccaṁ; tatra vo rāgo pahātabbo. ||| Consciousness is impermanent; you should give up greed for it.
Yaṁ, bhikkhave, aniccaṁ; tatra vo rāgo pahātabbo”ti. ||| You should give up greed for what is impermanent.”
Tatiyaṁ.
