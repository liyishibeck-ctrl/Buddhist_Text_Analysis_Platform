Saṁyutta Nikāya 17.31 ||| Linked Discourses 17.31
4. Catutthavagga ||| Chapter Four
Bhindisutta ||| Schism
Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Dāruṇo, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko. ||| “Possessions, honor, and popularity are grim …
Lābhasakkārasilokena abhibhūto pariyādiṇṇacitto, bhikkhave, devadatto saṅghaṁ bhindi. ||| Devadatta split the Saṅgha because his mind was overcome and overwhelmed by possessions, honor, and popularity.
Evaṁ dāruṇo kho, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko …pe… ||| So grim are possessions, honor, and popularity. …”
sikkhitabban”ti.
Paṭhamaṁ.
