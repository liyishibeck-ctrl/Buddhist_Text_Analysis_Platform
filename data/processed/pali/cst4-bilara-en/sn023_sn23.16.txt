Saṁyutta Nikāya 23.16 ||| Linked Discourses 23.16
2. Dutiyamāravagga ||| 2. About Māra (2nd)
Dukkhadhammasutta ||| Liable to Suffering
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho āyasmā rādho bhagavantaṁ etadavoca: ||| Seated to one side, Venerable Rādha said to the Buddha:
“‘dukkhadhammo, dukkhadhammo’ti, bhante, vuccati. ||| “Sir, they speak of having ‘suffering nature’.
Katamo nu kho, bhante, dukkhadhammo”ti? ||| What is of a nature to suffer?”
“Rūpaṁ kho, rādha, dukkhadhammo, vedanā dukkhadhammo, saññā dukkhadhammo, saṅkhārā dukkhadhammo, viññāṇaṁ dukkhadhammo. ||| “Rādha, form, feeling, perception, choices, and consciousness are of a nature to suffer.
Evaṁ passaṁ …pe… ||| Seeing this …
nāparaṁ itthattāyāti pajānātī”ti. ||| They understand: ‘… there is nothing further for this place.’”
Chaṭṭhaṁ.
