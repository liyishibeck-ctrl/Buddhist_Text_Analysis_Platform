Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.265 ||| Numbered Discourses 4.265
27. Kammapathavagga ||| 27. Ways of Performing Deeds
Adinnādāyīsutta ||| Stealing
“Catūhi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ niraye. ||| “Mendicants, someone with four qualities is placed in hell as if delivered there.
Katamehi catūhi? ||| What four?
Attanā ca adinnādāyī hoti, parañca adinnādāne samādapeti, adinnādāne ca samanuñño hoti, adinnādānassa ca vaṇṇaṁ bhāsati— ||| They themselves steal …
imehi kho …pe…. ||| Someone with four qualities is placed in heaven as if delivered there. …
Attanā ca adinnādānā paṭivirato hoti, parañca adinnādānā veramaṇiyā samādapeti, adinnādānā veramaṇiyā ca samanuñño hoti, adinnādānā veramaṇiyā ca vaṇṇaṁ bhāsati—imehi kho, bhikkhave …pe…. ||| They don’t themselves steal … ”
Dutiyaṁ.
