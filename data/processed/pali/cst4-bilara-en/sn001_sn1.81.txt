Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.81 ||| Linked Discourses 1.81
8. Chetvāvagga ||| 8. Slain
Araṇasutta ||| Free of Strife
“Kesūdha araṇā loke, ||| “Who in the world is free of strife?
kesaṁ vusitaṁ na nassati; ||| Whose life is not wasted?
Kedha icchaṁ parijānanti, ||| Who here completely understands desire?
kesaṁ bhojissiyaṁ sadā. ||| Who always lives as their own master?
Kiṁsu mātā pitā bhātā, ||| To whom do mother, father, and brothers
vandanti naṁ patiṭṭhitaṁ; ||| bow when they’re established?
Kiṁsu idha jātihīnaṁ, ||| Who here, though low born,
abhivādenti khattiyā”ti. ||| is bowed to even by aristocrats?”
“Samaṇīdha araṇā loke, ||| “Ascetics are free of strife in the world.
Samaṇānaṁ vusitaṁ na nassati; ||| The life of ascetics is not wasted.
Samaṇā icchaṁ parijānanti, ||| Ascetics completely understand desire.
Samaṇānaṁ bhojissiyaṁ sadā. ||| Ascetics always live as their own master.
Samaṇaṁ mātā pitā bhātā, ||| Mother, father, and brothers
vandanti naṁ patiṭṭhitaṁ; ||| bow to ascetics when they’re established.
Samaṇīdha jātihīnaṁ, ||| Even though an ascetic is low born,
abhivādenti khattiyā”ti. ||| they’re bowed to even by aristocrats.”
Chetvāvaggo aṭṭhamo.
Tassuddānaṁ
Chetvā rathañca cittañca,
vuṭṭhi bhītā najīrati;
Issaraṁ kāmaṁ pātheyyaṁ,
pajjoto araṇena cāti.
Devatāsaṁyuttaṁ samattaṁ. ||| The Linked Discourses on Deities are complete.
