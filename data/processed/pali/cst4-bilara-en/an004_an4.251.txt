Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.251 ||| Numbered Discourses 4.251
25. Āpattibhayavagga ||| 25. Perils of Offenses
Dutiyavohārasutta ||| Expressions (2nd)
“Cattārome, bhikkhave, ariyavohārā. ||| “Mendicants, there are these four noble expressions.
Katame cattāro? ||| What four?
Adiṭṭhe adiṭṭhavāditā, asute asutavāditā, amute amutavāditā, aviññāte aviññātavāditā— ||| Saying you haven’t seen, heard, thought, or known something, and you haven’t.
Ime kho, bhikkhave, cattāro ariyavohārā”ti. ||| These are the four noble expressions.”
Navamaṁ.
