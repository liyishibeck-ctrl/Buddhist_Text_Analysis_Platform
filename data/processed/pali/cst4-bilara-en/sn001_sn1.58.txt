Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.58 ||| Linked Discourses 1.58
6. Jarāvagga ||| 6. Old Age
Uppathasutta ||| Deviation
“Kiṁsu uppatho akkhāto, ||| “What’s declared to be a deviation?
kiṁsu rattindivakkhayo; ||| What is ending day and night?
Kiṁ malaṁ brahmacariyassa, ||| What’s the stain of chastity?
kiṁ sinānamanodakan”ti. ||| What’s the waterless bath?”
“Rāgo uppatho akkhāto, ||| “Lust is declared to be a deviation.
Vayo rattindivakkhayo; ||| Youth is ending day and night.
Itthī malaṁ brahmacariyassa, ||| Women are the stain of chastity,
Etthāyaṁ sajjate pajā; ||| to which this generation clings.
Tapo ca brahmacariyañca, ||| Fervor and chastity
Taṁ sinānamanodakan”ti. ||| are the waterless bath.”
