Aṅguttara Nikāya 7 ||| Numbered Discourses 7
11. Rāgapeyyāla ||| 11. Abbreviated Texts Beginning With Greed
~ ||| Untitled Discourse on Greed (2nd)
“Rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya satta dhammā bhāvetabbā. ||| “Mendicants, for insight into greed, seven things should be developed.
Katame satta? ||| What seven?
Aniccasaññā, anattasaññā, asubhasaññā, ādīnavasaññā, pahānasaññā, virāgasaññā, nirodhasaññā— ||| The perception of impermanence, the perception of not-self, the perception of ugliness, the perception of drawbacks, the perception of giving up, the perception of fading away, and the perception of cessation.
rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya ime satta dhammā bhāvetabbā”ti. ||| These seven things should be developed for insight into greed.”
