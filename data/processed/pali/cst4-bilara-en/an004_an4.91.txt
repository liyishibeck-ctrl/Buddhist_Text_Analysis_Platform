Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.91 ||| Numbered Discourses 4.91
10. Asuravagga ||| 10. Titans
Asurasutta ||| Titans
“Cattārome, bhikkhave, puggalā santo saṁvijjamānā lokasmiṁ. ||| “Mendicants, these four individuals are found in the world.
Katame cattāro? ||| What four?
Asuro asuraparivāro, ||| A titan surrounded by titans,
asuro devaparivāro, ||| a titan surrounded by gods,
devo asuraparivāro, ||| a god surrounded by titans, and
devo devaparivāro. ||| a god surrounded by gods.
Kathañca, bhikkhave, puggalo asuro hoti asuraparivāro? ||| And how is an individual a titan surrounded by titans?
Idha, bhikkhave, ekacco puggalo dussīlo hoti pāpadhammo, parisāpissa hoti dussīlā pāpadhammā. ||| It’s when an individual is unethical, of bad character, and their followers are the same.
Evaṁ kho, bhikkhave, puggalo asuro hoti asuraparivāro. ||| That’s how an individual is a titan surrounded by titans.
Kathañca, bhikkhave, puggalo asuro hoti devaparivāro? ||| And how is an individual a titan surrounded by gods?
Idha, bhikkhave, ekacco puggalo dussīlo hoti pāpadhammo, parisā ca khvassa hoti sīlavatī kalyāṇadhammā. ||| It’s when an individual is unethical, of bad character, but their followers are ethical, of good character.
Evaṁ kho, bhikkhave, puggalo asuro hoti devaparivāro. ||| That’s how an individual is a titan surrounded by gods.
Kathañca, bhikkhave, puggalo devo hoti asuraparivāro? ||| And how is an individual a god surrounded by titans?
Idha, bhikkhave, ekacco puggalo sīlavā hoti kalyāṇadhammo, parisā ca khvassa hoti dussīlā pāpadhammā. ||| It’s when an individual is ethical, of good character, but their followers are unethical, of bad character.
Evaṁ kho, bhikkhave, puggalo devo hoti asuraparivāro. ||| That’s how an individual is a god surrounded by titans.
Kathañca, bhikkhave, puggalo devo hoti devaparivāro? ||| And how is an individual a god surrounded by gods?
Idha, bhikkhave, ekacco puggalo sīlavā hoti kalyāṇadhammo, parisāpissa hoti sīlavatī kalyāṇadhammā. ||| It’s when an individual is ethical, of good character, and their followers are the same.
Evaṁ kho, bhikkhave, puggalo devo hoti, devaparivāro. ||| That’s how an individual is a god surrounded by gods.
Ime kho, bhikkhave, cattāro puggalā santo saṁvijjamānā lokasmin”ti. ||| These are the four individuals found in the world.”
Paṭhamaṁ.
