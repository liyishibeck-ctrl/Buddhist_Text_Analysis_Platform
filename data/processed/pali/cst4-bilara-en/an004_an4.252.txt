Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.252 ||| Numbered Discourses 4.252
25. Āpattibhayavagga ||| 25. Perils of Offenses
Tatiyavohārasutta ||| Expressions (3rd)
“Cattārome, bhikkhave, anariyavohārā. ||| “Mendicants, there are these four ignoble expressions.
Katame cattāro? ||| What four?
Diṭṭhe adiṭṭhavāditā, sute asutavāditā, mute amutavāditā, viññāte aviññātavāditā— ||| Saying you haven’t seen, heard, thought, or known something, and you have.
Ime kho, bhikkhave, cattāro anariyavohārā”ti. ||| These are the four ignoble expressions.”
Dasamaṁ.
