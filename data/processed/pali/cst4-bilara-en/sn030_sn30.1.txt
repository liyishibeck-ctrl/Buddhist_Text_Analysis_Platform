Saṁyutta Nikāya 30.1 ||| Linked Discourses 30.1
1. Supaṇṇavagga ||| 1. Phoenixes
Suddhikasutta ||| Plain Version
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Catasso imā, bhikkhave, supaṇṇayoniyo. ||| “Mendicants, phoenixes reproduce in these four ways.
Katamā catasso? ||| What four?
Aṇḍajā supaṇṇā, jalābujā supaṇṇā, saṁsedajā supaṇṇā, opapātikā supaṇṇā— ||| Phoenixes are born from eggs, from a womb, from moisture, or spontaneously.
imā kho, bhikkhave, catasso supaṇṇayoniyo”ti. ||| These are the four ways that phoenixes reproduce.”
Paṭhamaṁ.
