Aṅguttara Nikāya 6.76 ||| Numbered Discourses 6.76
8. Arahattavagga ||| 8. Perfection
Arahattasutta ||| Perfection
“Cha, bhikkhave, dhamme appahāya abhabbo arahattaṁ sacchikātuṁ. ||| “Mendicants, without giving up six things you can’t realize perfection.
Katame cha? ||| What six?
Mānaṁ, omānaṁ, atimānaṁ, adhimānaṁ, thambhaṁ, atinipātaṁ. ||| Conceit, inferiority complex, superiority complex, overestimation, obstinacy, and groveling.
Ime kho, bhikkhave, cha dhamme appahāya abhabbo arahattaṁ sacchikātuṁ. ||| Without giving up these six qualities you can’t realize perfection.
Cha, bhikkhave, dhamme pahāya bhabbo arahattaṁ sacchikātuṁ. ||| After giving up six things you can realize perfection.
Katame cha? ||| What six?
Mānaṁ, omānaṁ, atimānaṁ, adhimānaṁ, thambhaṁ, atinipātaṁ. ||| Conceit, inferiority complex, superiority complex, overestimation, obstinacy, and groveling.
Ime kho, bhikkhave, cha dhamme pahāya bhabbo arahattaṁ sacchikātun”ti. ||| After giving up these six things you can realize perfection.”
Dutiyaṁ.
