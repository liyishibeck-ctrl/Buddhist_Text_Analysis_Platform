Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.27 ||| Linked Discourses 1.27
3. Sattivagga ||| 3. A Sword
Sarasutta ||| Streams
“Kuto sarā nivattanti, ||| “From where do streams recoil?
kattha vaṭṭaṁ na vattati; ||| Where does the cycle spin no more?
Kattha nāmañca rūpañca, ||| Where does name and form
asesaṁ uparujjhatī”ti. ||| cease with no residue left behind?”
“Yattha āpo ca pathavī, ||| “Where water and earth,
tejo vāyo na gādhati; ||| fire and air have no footing—
Ato sarā nivattanti, ||| from here the streams recoil;
ettha vaṭṭaṁ na vattati; ||| in reference to this, the cycle spins no more;
Ettha nāmañca rūpañca, ||| in reference to this, name and form
asesaṁ uparujjhatī”ti. ||| ceases with no residue left behind.”
