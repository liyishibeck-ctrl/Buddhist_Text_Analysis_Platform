Saṁyutta Nikāya 37.12 ||| Linked Discourses 37.12
1. Paṭhamapeyyālavagga ||| 1. Abbreviated Texts
Kusītasutta ||| Lazy
“Pañcahi, anuruddha, dhammehi samannāgato mātugāmo …pe… nirayaṁ upapajjati.
Katamehi pañcahi?
Assaddho ca hoti, ahiriko ca hoti, anottappī ca hoti, kusīto ca hoti, duppañño ca hoti— ||| “… They’re faithless, shameless, imprudent, lazy, and witless. …”
imehi kho, anuruddha, pañcahi dhammehi samannāgato mātugāmo …pe… apāyaṁ duggatiṁ vinipātaṁ nirayaṁ upapajjatī”ti.
Dvādasamaṁ.
