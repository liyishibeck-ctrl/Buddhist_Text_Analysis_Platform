Saṁyutta Nikāya 45.22 ||| Linked Discourses 45.22
3. Micchattavagga ||| 3. The Wrong Way
Akusaladhammasutta ||| Unskillful Qualities
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Akusale ca kho, bhikkhave, dhamme desessāmi, kusale ca dhamme. ||| “Mendicants, I will teach you skillful and unskillful qualities.
Taṁ suṇātha. ||| Listen …
Katame ca, bhikkhave, akusalā dhammā? ||| And what are unskillful qualities?
Seyyathidaṁ—micchādiṭṭhi …pe… micchāsamādhi. ||| They are wrong view, wrong purpose, wrong speech, wrong action, wrong livelihood, wrong effort, wrong mindfulness, and wrong immersion.
Ime vuccanti, bhikkhave, akusalā dhammā. ||| These are called unskillful qualities.
Katame ca, bhikkhave, kusalā dhammā? ||| And what are skillful qualities?
Seyyathidaṁ—sammādiṭṭhi …pe… sammāsamādhi. ||| They are right view, right purpose, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, and right immersion.
Ime vuccanti, bhikkhave, kusalā dhammā”ti. ||| These are called skillful qualities.”
Dutiyaṁ.
