Saṁyutta Nikāya 17.33 ||| Linked Discourses 17.33
4. Catutthavagga ||| Chapter Four
Kusaladhammasutta ||| Skillful Quality
Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Dāruṇo, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko. ||| “Possessions, honor, and popularity are grim …
Lābhasakkārasilokena abhibhūtassa pariyādiṇṇacittassa, bhikkhave, devadattassa kusalo dhammo samucchedamagamā. ||| Devadatta cut off his skillful quality because his mind was overcome and overwhelmed by possessions, honor, and popularity.
Evaṁ dāruṇo kho, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko …pe… ||| So grim are possessions, honor, and popularity. …”
sikkhitabban”ti.
Tatiyaṁ.
