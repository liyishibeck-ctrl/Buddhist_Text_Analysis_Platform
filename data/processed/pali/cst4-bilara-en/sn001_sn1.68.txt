Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.68 ||| Linked Discourses 1.68
7. Addhavagga ||| 7. Overcome
Pihitasutta ||| Fastened Shut
“Kenassu pihito loko, ||| “By what is the world fastened shut?
kismiṁ loko patiṭṭhito; ||| On what is the world grounded?
Kenassu uḍḍito loko, ||| By what is the world ensnared?
kenassu parivārito”ti. ||| By what is it surrounded?”
“Maccunā pihito loko, ||| “The world is fastened shut by mortality.
dukkhe loko patiṭṭhito; ||| The world is grounded on suffering.
Taṇhāya uḍḍito loko, ||| Craving has ensnared the world.
jarāya parivārito”ti. ||| It’s surrounded by old age.”
