Saṁyutta Nikāya 43.4 ||| Linked Discourses 43.4
1. Paṭhamavagga ||| Chapter One
Suññatasamādhisutta ||| Emptiness Immersion
“Katamo ca, bhikkhave, asaṅkhatagāmimaggo? ||| “And what is the path that leads to the unconditioned?
Suññato samādhi, animitto samādhi, appaṇihito samādhi—ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, asaṅkhatagāmimaggo …pe…. ||| Emptiness immersion; signless immersion; undirected immersion. …”
Catutthaṁ.
