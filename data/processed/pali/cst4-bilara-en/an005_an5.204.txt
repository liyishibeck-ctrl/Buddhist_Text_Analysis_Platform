Aṅguttara Nikāya 5.204 ||| Numbered Discourses 5.204
21. Kimilavagga ||| 21. With Kimbila
Balasutta ||| Powers
“Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, balāni. ||| “Mendicants, there are these five powers.
Katamāni pañca? ||| What five?
Saddhābalaṁ, hiribalaṁ, ottappabalaṁ, vīriyabalaṁ, paññābalaṁ— ||| Faith, conscience, prudence, energy, and wisdom.
imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañca balānī”ti. ||| These are the five powers.”
Catutthaṁ.
