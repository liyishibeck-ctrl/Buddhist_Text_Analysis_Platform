Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.22 ||| Linked Discourses 1.22
3. Sattivagga ||| 3. A Sword
Phusatisutta ||| Impact
“Nāphusantaṁ phusati ca, ||| “It doesn’t impact one who doesn’t impact others.
phusantañca tato phuse; ||| It impacts one because they impact others.
Tasmā phusantaṁ phusati, ||| That’s why it impacts one who impacts,
appaduṭṭhapadosinan”ti. ||| who wrongs one who’s done no wrong.”
“Yo appaduṭṭhassa narassa dussati, ||| “Whoever wrongs a man who has done no wrong,
Suddhassa posassa anaṅgaṇassa; ||| a pure man who has not a blemish,
Tameva bālaṁ pacceti pāpaṁ, ||| the evil backfires on the fool,
Sukhumo rajo paṭivātaṁva khitto”ti. ||| like fine dust thrown upwind.”
