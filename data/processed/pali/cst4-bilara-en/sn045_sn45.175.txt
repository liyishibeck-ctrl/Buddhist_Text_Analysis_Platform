Saṁyutta Nikāya 45.175 ||| Linked Discourses 45.175
14. Oghavagga ||| 14. Floods
Anusayasutta ||| Tendencies
“Sattime, bhikkhave, anusayā. ||| “Mendicants, there are these seven underlying tendencies.
Katame satta? ||| What seven?
Kāmarāgānusayo, paṭighānusayo, diṭṭhānusayo, vicikicchānusayo, mānānusayo, bhavarāgānusayo, avijjānusayo— ||| The underlying tendencies of sensual desire, aversion, views, doubt, conceit, desire for continued existence, and ignorance.
ime kho, bhikkhave, sattānusayā. ||| These are the seven underlying tendencies.
Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, sattannaṁ anusayānaṁ abhiññāya pariññāya parikkhayāya pahānāya …pe… ayaṁ ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo bhāvetabbo”ti. ||| The noble eightfold path should be developed for the direct knowledge, complete understanding, finishing, and giving up of these seven underlying tendencies.”
Pañcamaṁ.
