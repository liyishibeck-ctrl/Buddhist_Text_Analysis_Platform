Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.149 ||| Numbered Discourses 4.149
15. Ābhāvagga ||| 15. Brightness
Sucaritasutta ||| Good Conduct
“Cattārimāni, bhikkhave, vacīsucaritāni. ||| “Mendicants, there are these four kinds of good conduct by way of speech.
Katamāni cattāri? ||| What four?
Saccavācā, apisuṇā vācā, saṇhā vācā, mantabhāsā— ||| Speech that’s true, harmonious, gentle, and thoughtful.
Imāni kho, bhikkhave, cattāri vacīsucaritānī”ti. ||| These are the four kinds of good conduct by way of speech.”
Navamaṁ.
