Aṅguttara Nikāya 7.10 ||| Numbered Discourses 7.10
1. Dhanavagga ||| 1. Wealth
Macchariyasutta ||| Stinginess
“Sattimāni, bhikkhave, saṁyojanāni. ||| “Mendicants, there are these seven fetters.
Katamāni satta? ||| What seven?
Anunayasaṁyojanaṁ, paṭighasaṁyojanaṁ, diṭṭhisaṁyojanaṁ, vicikicchāsaṁyojanaṁ, mānasaṁyojanaṁ, issāsaṁyojanaṁ, macchariyasaṁyojanaṁ. ||| The fetters of attraction, aversion, views, doubt, conceit, jealousy, and stinginess.
Imāni kho, bhikkhave, satta saṁyojanānī”ti. ||| These are the seven fetters.”
Dasamaṁ.
Dhanavaggo paṭhamo.
Tassuddānaṁ
Dve piyāni balaṁ dhanaṁ,
Saṅkhittañceva vitthataṁ;
Uggaṁ saṁyojanañceva,
Pahānaṁ macchariyena cāti.
