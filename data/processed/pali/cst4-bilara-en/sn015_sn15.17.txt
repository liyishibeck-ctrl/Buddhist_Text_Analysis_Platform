Saṁyutta Nikāya 15.17 ||| Linked Discourses 15.17
2. Dutiyavagga ||| Chapter Two
Bhaginisutta ||| Sister
Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati …pe… ||| At Sāvatthī.
“na so, bhikkhave, satto sulabharūpo yo nabhaginibhūtapubbo …pe… ||| “It’s not easy to find a sentient being who in all this long time has not previously been your sister. …
alaṁ vimuccitun”ti. ||| This is quite enough for you to become disillusioned, dispassionate, and freed regarding all conditions.”
Sattamaṁ.
