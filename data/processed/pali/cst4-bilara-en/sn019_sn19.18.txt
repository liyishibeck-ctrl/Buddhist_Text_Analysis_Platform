Pāpabhikkhunīsutta ||| A Bad Nun
“Addasaṁ bhikkhuniṁ vehāsaṁ gacchantiṁ. ||| “I saw a nun flying through the air.

pāpabhikkhunī ahosi …pe…. ||| “She used to be a bad nun …”
