Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.54 ||| Linked Discourses 1.54
6. Jarāvagga ||| 6. Old Age
Vatthusutta ||| Grounds
“Kiṁsu vatthu manussānaṁ, ||| “What is the site for human beings?
kiṁsūdha paramo sakhā; ||| What is the best comrade here?
Kiṁsu bhūtā upajīvanti, ||| By what do the creatures who live off the earth
ye pāṇā pathavissitā”ti. ||| sustain their life?”
“Puttā vatthu manussānaṁ, ||| “Children are the site for human beings.
bhariyā ca paramo sakhā; ||| A wife is the best comrade.
Vuṭṭhiṁ bhūtā upajīvanti, ||| The creatures who live off the earth
ye pāṇā pathavissitā”ti. ||| sustain their life by rain.”
