Aṅguttara Nikāya 7.18 ||| Numbered Discourses 7.18
2. Anusayavagga ||| 2. Tendencies
Anattānupassīsutta ||| Observing Not-self
Sabbesu dhammesu anattānupassī viharati …pe…. ||| “First, take an individual who meditates observing not-self in all things. They perceive not-self and experience not-self. Constantly, continually, and without interruption, they apply the mind and fathom with wisdom. …”
Aṭṭhamaṁ.
