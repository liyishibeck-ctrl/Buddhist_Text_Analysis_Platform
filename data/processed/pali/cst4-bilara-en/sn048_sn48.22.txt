Saṁyutta Nikāya 48.22 ||| Linked Discourses 48.22
3. Chaḷindriyavagga ||| 3. The Six Faculties
Jīvitindriyasutta ||| The Life Faculty
“Tīṇimāni, bhikkhave, indriyāni. ||| “Mendicants, there are these three faculties.
Katamāni tīṇi? ||| What three?
Itthindriyaṁ, purisindriyaṁ, jīvitindriyaṁ— ||| The faculties of femininity, masculinity, and life.
imāni kho, bhikkhave, tīṇi indriyānī”ti. ||| These are the three faculties.”
Dutiyaṁ.
