Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.261 ||| Numbered Discourses 4.261
26. Abhiññāvagga ||| 26. Insight
Balasutta ||| Powers
“Cattārimāni, bhikkhave, balāni. ||| “Mendicants, there are these four powers.
Katamāni cattāri? ||| What four?
Vīriyabalaṁ, satibalaṁ, samādhibalaṁ, paññābalaṁ— ||| The powers of energy, mindfulness, immersion, and wisdom.
Imāni kho, bhikkhave, cattāri balānī”ti. ||| These are the four powers.”
Aṭṭhamaṁ.
