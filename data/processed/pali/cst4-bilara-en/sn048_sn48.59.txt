Saṁyutta Nikāya 48.59 ||| Linked Discourses 48.59
6. Sūkarakhatavagga ||| 6. The Boar’s Cave
Paṭhamauppādasutta ||| Arising (1st)
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, indriyāni bhāvitāni bahulīkatāni anuppannāni uppajjanti, nāññatra tathāgatassa pātubhāvā arahato sammāsambuddhassa. ||| “Mendicants, these five faculties don’t arise to be developed and cultivated except when a Realized One, a perfected one, a fully awakened Buddha has appeared.
Katamāni pañca? ||| What five?
Saddhindriyaṁ, vīriyindriyaṁ, satindriyaṁ, samādhindriyaṁ, paññindriyaṁ— ||| The faculties of faith, energy, mindfulness, immersion, and wisdom.
imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañcindriyāni bhāvitāni bahulīkatāni anuppannāni uppajjanti, nāññatra tathāgatassa pātubhāvā arahato sammāsambuddhassā”ti. ||| These five faculties don’t arise to be developed and cultivated except when a Realized One, a perfected one, a fully awakened Buddha has appeared.”
Navamaṁ.
