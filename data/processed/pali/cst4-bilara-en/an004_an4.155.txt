Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.155 ||| Numbered Discourses 4.155
16. Indriyavagga ||| 16. Faculties
Paṭisaṅkhānabalasutta ||| The Power of Reflection
“Cattārimāni, bhikkhave, balāni. ||| “Mendicants, there are these four powers.
Katamāni cattāri? ||| What four?
Paṭisaṅkhānabalaṁ, bhāvanābalaṁ, anavajjabalaṁ, saṅgahabalaṁ— ||| The powers of reflection, development, blamelessness, and inclusiveness.
Imāni kho, bhikkhave, cattāri balānī”ti. ||| These are the four powers.”
Pañcamaṁ.
