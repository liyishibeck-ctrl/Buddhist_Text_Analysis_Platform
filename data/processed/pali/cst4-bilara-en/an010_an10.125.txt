Aṅguttara Nikāya 10.125 ||| Numbered Discourses 10.125
13. Parisuddhavagga ||| 13. Purified
Tatiyasutta ||| Third
“Dasayime, bhikkhave, dhammā mahapphalā mahānisaṁsā, nāññatra sugatavinayā. ||| “Mendicants, these ten things are not very fruitful and beneficial apart from the Holy One’s training.
Katame dasa? ||| What ten?
Sammādiṭṭhi …pe… sammāvimutti— ||| Right view, right purpose, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, right immersion, right knowledge, and right freedom.
ime kho, bhikkhave, dasa dhammā mahapphalā mahānisaṁsā, nāññatra sugatavinayā”ti. ||| These are the ten things that are not very fruitful and beneficial apart from the Holy One’s training.”
Tatiyaṁ.
