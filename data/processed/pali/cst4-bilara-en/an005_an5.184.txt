Aṅguttara Nikāya 5.184 ||| Numbered Discourses 5.184
19. Araññavagga ||| 19. Wilderness Dwellers
Sosānikasutta ||| Charnel Ground Dwellers
“Pañcime, bhikkhave, sosānikā. ||| “Mendicants, these five dwell in a charnel ground.
Katame pañca? ||| What five?
Mandattā momūhattā sosāniko hoti …pe… idamatthitaṁyeva nissāya sosāniko hoti. ||| One may dwell in a charnel ground because of stupidity … corrupt wishes … madness … because it is praised by the Buddhas … or for the sake of having few wishes …
Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca sosānikā”ti. ||| These are the five who dwell in a charnel ground.”
Catutthaṁ.
