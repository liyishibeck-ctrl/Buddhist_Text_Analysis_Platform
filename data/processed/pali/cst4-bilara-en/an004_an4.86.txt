Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.86 ||| Numbered Discourses 4.86
9. Macalavagga ||| 9. Confirmed
Oṇatoṇatasutta ||| Sunk Low
“Cattārome, bhikkhave, puggalā santo saṁvijjamānā lokasmiṁ. ||| “These four individuals are found in the world.
Katame cattāro? ||| What four?
Oṇatoṇato, ||| One sunk low who sinks lower,
oṇatuṇṇato, ||| one sunk low who rises high,
uṇṇatoṇato, ||| one risen high who sinks low, and
uṇṇatuṇṇato. ||| one risen high who rises higher.
… Ime kho, bhikkhave, cattāro puggalā santo saṁvijjamānā lokasmin”ti. ||| … These are the four individuals found in the world.”
Chaṭṭhaṁ.
