Saṁyutta Nikāya 48.60 ||| Linked Discourses 48.60
6. Sūkarakhatavagga ||| 6. The Boar’s Cave
Dutiyauppādasutta ||| Arising (2nd)
“Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, indriyāni bhāvitāni bahulīkatāni anuppannāni uppajjanti, nāññatra sugatavinayā. ||| “Mendicants, these five faculties don’t arise to be developed and cultivated apart from the Holy One’s training.
Katamāni pañca? ||| What five?
Saddhindriyaṁ, vīriyindriyaṁ, satindriyaṁ, samādhindriyaṁ, paññindriyaṁ— ||| The faculties of faith, energy, mindfulness, immersion, and wisdom.
imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañcindriyāni bhāvitāni bahulīkatāni anuppannāni uppajjanti, nāññatra sugatavinayā”ti. ||| These five faculties don’t arise to be developed and cultivated apart from the Holy One’s training.”
Dasamaṁ.
Sūkarakhatavaggo chaṭṭho.
Tassuddānaṁ
Sālaṁ mallikaṁ sekho ca,
padaṁ sāraṁ patiṭṭhitaṁ;
Brahmasūkarakhatāyo,
uppādā apare duveti.
