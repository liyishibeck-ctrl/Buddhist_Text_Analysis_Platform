Aṅguttara Nikāya 6 ||| Numbered Discourses 6
13. Rāgapeyyāla ||| 13. Abbreviated Texts Beginning with Greed
~ ||| Untitled Discourse on Greed (3rd)
“Rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya cha dhammā bhāvetabbā. ||| “For insight into greed, six things should be developed.
Katame cha? ||| What six?
Aniccasaññā, anicce dukkhasaññā, dukkhe anattasaññā, pahānasaññā, virāgasaññā, nirodhasaññā. ||| The perception of impermanence, the perception of suffering in impermanence, the perception of not-self in suffering, the perception of giving up, the perception of fading away, and the perception of cessation.
Rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya ime cha dhammā bhāvetabbā”ti. ||| For insight into greed, these six things should be developed.”
