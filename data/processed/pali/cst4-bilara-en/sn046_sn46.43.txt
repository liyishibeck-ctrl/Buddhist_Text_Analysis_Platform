Saṁyutta Nikāya 46.43 ||| Linked Discourses 46.43
5. Cakkavattivagga ||| 5. A Wheel-Turning Monarch
Mārasutta ||| About Māra
“Mārasenappamaddanaṁ vo, bhikkhave, maggaṁ desessāmi; ||| “Mendicants, I will teach you a path for crushing Māra’s army.
taṁ suṇātha. ||| Listen …
Katamo ca, bhikkhave, mārasenappamaddano maggo? ||| And what is that path?
Yadidaṁ—
satta bojjhaṅgā. ||| It is the seven awakening factors.
Katame satta? ||| What seven?
Satisambojjhaṅgo …pe… upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo— ||| The awakening factors of mindfulness, investigation of principles, energy, rapture, tranquility, immersion, and equanimity.
ayaṁ kho, bhikkhave, mārasenappamaddano maggo”ti. ||| This is the path for crushing Māra’s army.”
Tatiyaṁ.
