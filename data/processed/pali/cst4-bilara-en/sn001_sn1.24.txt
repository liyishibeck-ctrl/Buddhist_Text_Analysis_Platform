Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.24 ||| Linked Discourses 1.24
3. Sattivagga ||| 3. A Sword
Manonivāraṇasutta ||| Shielding the Mind
“Yato yato mano nivāraye, ||| “Whatever you’ve shielded the mind from
Na dukkhameti naṁ tato tato; ||| can’t cause you suffering.
Sa sabbato mano nivāraye, ||| So you should shield the mind from everything,
Sa sabbato dukkhā pamuccatī”ti. ||| then you’re freed from all suffering.”
“Na sabbato mano nivāraye, ||| “You need not shield the mind from everything,
Na mano saṁyatattamāgataṁ; ||| nor has the mind gained self-control.
Yato yato ca pāpakaṁ, ||| The mind need only be shielded
Tato tato mano nivāraye”ti. ||| from where the bad things come.”
