Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.151 ||| Numbered Discourses 4.151
16. Indriyavagga ||| 16. Faculties
Indriyasutta ||| Faculties
“Cattārimāni, bhikkhave, indriyāni. ||| “Mendicants, there are these four faculties.
Katamāni cattāri? ||| What four?
Saddhindriyaṁ, vīriyindriyaṁ, satindriyaṁ, samādhindriyaṁ— ||| The faculties of faith, energy, mindfulness, and immersion.
Imāni kho, bhikkhave, cattāri indriyānī”ti. ||| These are the four faculties.”
Paṭhamaṁ.
