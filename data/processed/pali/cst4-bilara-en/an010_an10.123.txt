Aṅguttara Nikāya 10.123 ||| Numbered Discourses 10.123
13. Parisuddhavagga ||| 13. Purified
Paṭhamasutta ||| First
“Dasayime, bhikkhave, dhammā parisuddhā pariyodātā, nāññatra sugatavinayā. ||| “Mendicants, these ten things are not purified and cleansed apart from the Holy One’s training.
Katame dasa? ||| What ten?
Sammādiṭṭhi, sammāsaṅkappo, sammāvācā, sammākammanto, sammāājīvo, sammāvāyāmo, sammāsati, sammāsamādhi, sammāñāṇaṁ, sammāvimutti— ||| Right view, right purpose, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, right immersion, right knowledge, and right freedom.
ime kho, bhikkhave, dasa dhammā parisuddhā pariyodātā, nāññatra sugatavinayā”ti. ||| These ten things are not purified and cleansed apart from the Holy One’s training.”
Paṭhamaṁ.
