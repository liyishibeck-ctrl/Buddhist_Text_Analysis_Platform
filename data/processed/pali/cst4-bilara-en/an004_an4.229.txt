Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.229 ||| Numbered Discourses 4.229
23. Duccaritavagga ||| 23. Bad Conduct
Ahirikasutta ||| Lack of Conscience
…pe… Assaddho hoti, dussīlo hoti, ahiriko hoti, anottappī hoti …pe… saddho hoti, sīlavā hoti, hirimā hoti, ottappī hoti …pe…. ||| “A foolish person … brims with much wickedness. … They’re faithless, unethical, shameless, and imprudent. … An astute person … brims with much merit. … They’re faithful, ethical, conscientious, and prudent. …”
Navamaṁ.
