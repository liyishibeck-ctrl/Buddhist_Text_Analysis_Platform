Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.153 ||| Numbered Discourses 4.153
16. Indriyavagga ||| 16. Faculties
Paññābalasutta ||| The Power of Wisdom
“Cattārimāni, bhikkhave, balāni. ||| “Mendicants, there are these four powers.
Katamāni cattāri? ||| What four?
Paññābalaṁ, vīriyabalaṁ, anavajjabalaṁ, saṅgahabalaṁ— ||| The powers of wisdom, energy, blamelessness, and inclusiveness.
Imāni kho, bhikkhave, cattāri balānī”ti. ||| These are the four powers.”
Tatiyaṁ.
