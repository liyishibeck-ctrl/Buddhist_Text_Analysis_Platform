Saṁyutta Nikāya 45.38 ||| Linked Discourses 45.38
4. Paṭipattivagga ||| 4. Practice
Dutiyabrahmaññasutta ||| The Brahmin Life (2nd)
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Brahmaññañca vo, bhikkhave, desessāmi, brahmaññatthañca. ||| “Mendicants, I will teach you life as a brahmin and the goal of life as a brahmin.
Taṁ suṇātha. ||| Listen …
Katamañca, bhikkhave, brahmaññaṁ? ||| And what is life as a brahmin?
Ayameva ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo, seyyathidaṁ— ||| It is simply this noble eightfold path, that is:
sammādiṭṭhi …pe… sammāsamādhi. ||| right view, right purpose, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, and right immersion.
Idaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, brahmaññaṁ. ||| This is called life as a brahmin.
Katamo ca, bhikkhave, brahmaññattho? ||| And what is the goal of life as a brahmin?
Yo kho, bhikkhave, rāgakkhayo dosakkhayo mohakkhayo— ||| The ending of greed, hate, and delusion.
ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, brahmaññattho”ti. ||| This is called the goal of life as a brahmin.”
Aṭṭhamaṁ.
