Saṁyutta Nikāya 46.66 ||| Linked Discourses 46.66
7. Ānāpānavagga ||| 7. Breathing
Ānāpānasutta ||| Breathing
“Ānāpānassati, bhikkhave, bhāvitā …pe…” ||| “Mendicants, when mindfulness of breathing is developed and cultivated it’s very fruitful and beneficial. …”
dasamaṁ.
Ānāpānavaggo sattamo.
Tassuddānaṁ
Aṭṭhikapuḷavakaṁ vinīlakaṁ,
Vicchiddakaṁ uddhumātena pañcamaṁ;
Mettā karuṇā muditā,
Upekkhā ānāpānena te dasāti.
