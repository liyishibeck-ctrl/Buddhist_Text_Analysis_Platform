Saṁyutta Nikāya 23.12 ||| Linked Discourses 23.12
2. Dutiyamāravagga ||| 2. About Māra (2nd)
Māradhammasutta ||| Subject to Māra
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho āyasmā rādho bhagavantaṁ etadavoca: ||| Seated to one side, Venerable Rādha said to the Buddha:
“‘māradhammo, māradhammo’ti, bhante, vuccati. ||| “Sir, they speak of ‘Māra nature’.
Katamo nu kho, bhante, māradhammo”ti? ||| What is of Māra nature?”
“Rūpaṁ kho, rādha, māradhammo, vedanā māradhammo, saññā māradhammo, saṅkhārā māradhammo, viññāṇaṁ māradhammo. ||| “Rādha, form is of Māra nature. Feeling, perception, choices, and consciousness are of Māra nature.
Evaṁ passaṁ …pe… ||| Seeing this …
nāparaṁ itthattāyāti pajānātī”ti. ||| They understand: ‘… there is nothing further for this place.’”
Dutiyaṁ.
