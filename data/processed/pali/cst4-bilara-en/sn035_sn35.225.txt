17. Saṭṭhipeyyālavagga ||| 17. Sixty Abbreviated Texts
Bāhirāyatanaaniccasutta ||| The Exterior as Impermanent
“Rūpā, bhikkhave, aniccā. Saddā … gandhā … rasā … phoṭṭhabbā … dhammā aniccā. ||| “Mendicants, sights, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, and ideas are impermanent.
Evaṁ passaṁ …pe… ||| Seeing this …
nāparaṁ itthattāyāti pajānātī”ti. ||| They understand: ‘… there is nothing further for this place.’”
