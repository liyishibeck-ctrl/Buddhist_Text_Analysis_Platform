Aṅguttara Nikāya 2 ||| Numbered Discourses 2.64–76
7. Sukhavagga ||| The Chapter on Happiness
64 ||| 64
“Dvemāni, bhikkhave, sukhāni. ||| “There are, mendicants, these two kinds of happiness.
Katamāni dve? ||| What two?
Gihisukhañca pabbajitasukhañca. ||| The happiness of laypeople, and the happiness of renunciates.
Imāni kho, bhikkhave, dve sukhāni. ||| These are the two kinds of happiness.
Etadaggaṁ, bhikkhave, imesaṁ dvinnaṁ sukhānaṁ yadidaṁ pabbajitasukhan”ti. ||| The better of these two kinds of happiness is the happiness of renunciates.”
