Aṅguttara Nikāya 5.202 ||| Numbered Discourses 5.202
21. Kimilavagga ||| 21. With Kimbila
Dhammassavanasutta ||| Listening to the Teaching
“Pañcime, bhikkhave, ānisaṁsā dhammassavane. ||| “Mendicants, there are these five benefits of listening to the teaching.
Katame pañca? ||| What five?
Assutaṁ suṇāti, sutaṁ pariyodāpeti, kaṅkhaṁ vitarati, diṭṭhiṁ ujuṁ karoti, cittamassa pasīdati. ||| You learn new things, clarify what you’ve learned, get over uncertainty, correct your views, and inspire confidence in your mind.
Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca ānisaṁsā dhammassavane”ti. ||| These are the five benefits of listening to the teaching.”
Dutiyaṁ.
