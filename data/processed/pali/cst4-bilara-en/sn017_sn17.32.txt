Saṁyutta Nikāya 17.32 ||| Linked Discourses 17.32
4. Catutthavagga ||| Chapter Four
Kusalamūlasutta ||| Skillful Root
Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Dāruṇo, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko. ||| “Possessions, honor, and popularity are grim …
Lābhasakkārasilokena abhibhūtassa pariyādiṇṇacittassa, bhikkhave, devadattassa kusalamūlaṁ samucchedamagamā. ||| Devadatta cut off his skillful root because his mind was overcome and overwhelmed by possessions, honor, and popularity.
Evaṁ dāruṇo kho, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko …pe… ||| So grim are possessions, honor, and popularity. …”
sikkhitabban”ti.
Dutiyaṁ.
