Aṅguttara Nikāya 5.253 ||| Numbered Discourses 5.253
26. Upasampadāvagga ||| 26. Ordination
Sāmaṇerasutta ||| Who Should Have a Novice as Attendant
“Pañcahi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgatena bhikkhunā sāmaṇero upaṭṭhāpetabbo. ||| “Mendicants, a novice should attend on a mendicant with five qualities.
Katamehi pañcahi? ||| What five?
Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu asekhena sīlakkhandhena samannāgato hoti; asekhena samādhikkhandhena … asekhena paññākkhandhena … asekhena vimuttikkhandhena … asekhena vimuttiñāṇadassanakkhandhena samannāgato hoti. ||| It’s a mendicant who has the entire spectrum of an adept’s ethics, immersion, wisdom, freedom, and the knowledge and vision of freedom.
Imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi dhammehi samannāgatena bhikkhunā sāmaṇero upaṭṭhāpetabbo”ti. ||| A novice should attend on a mendicant with these five qualities.”
Tatiyaṁ.
