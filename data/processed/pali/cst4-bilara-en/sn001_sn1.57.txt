Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.57 ||| Linked Discourses 1.57
6. Jarāvagga ||| 6. Old Age
Tatiyajanasutta ||| Gives Birth (3rd)
“Kiṁsu janeti purisaṁ, ||| “What gives birth to a person?
kiṁsu tassa vidhāvati; ||| What do they have that runs about?
Kiṁsu saṁsāramāpādi, ||| What enters transmigration?
kiṁsu tassa parāyanan”ti. ||| What is their destiny?”
“Taṇhā janeti purisaṁ, ||| “Craving gives birth to a person.
cittamassa vidhāvati; ||| Their mind is what runs about.
Satto saṁsāramāpādi, ||| A sentient being enters transmigration.
kammaṁ tassa parāyanan”ti. ||| Deeds are their final haven.”
