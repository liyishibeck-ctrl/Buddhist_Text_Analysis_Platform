Saṁyutta Nikāya 22.32 ||| Linked Discourses 22.32
3. Bhāravagga ||| 3. The Burden
Pabhaṅgusutta ||| Brittle
Sāvatthinidānaṁ. ||| At Sāvatthī.
“Pabhaṅguñca, bhikkhave, desessāmi appabhaṅguñca. ||| “Mendicants, I will teach you what is brittle and what is not brittle.
Taṁ suṇātha. ||| Listen …
Kiñca, bhikkhave, pabhaṅgu, kiṁ appabhaṅgu? ||| And what is brittle? What is not brittle?
Rūpaṁ, bhikkhave, pabhaṅgu. ||| Form is brittle,
Yo tassa nirodho vūpasamo atthaṅgamo, idaṁ appabhaṅgu. ||| but its cessation, settling, and ending is not brittle.
Vedanā pabhaṅgu. ||| Feeling …
Yo tassā nirodho vūpasamo atthaṅgamo, idaṁ appabhaṅgu.
Saññā pabhaṅgu … ||| perception …
saṅkhārā pabhaṅgu. ||| choices …
Yo tesaṁ nirodho vūpasamo atthaṅgamo, idaṁ appabhaṅgu.
Viññāṇaṁ pabhaṅgu. ||| consciousness is brittle,
Yo tassa nirodho vūpasamo atthaṅgamo, idaṁ appabhaṅgū”ti. ||| but its cessation, settling, and ending is not brittle.”
Ekādasamaṁ.
Bhāravaggo tatiyo.
Tassuddānaṁ
Bhāraṁ pariññaṁ abhijānaṁ,
chandarāgaṁ catutthakaṁ;
Assādā ca tayo vuttā,
abhinandanamaṭṭhamaṁ;
Uppādaṁ aghamūlañca,
ekādasamo pabhaṅgūti.
