Aṅguttara Nikāya 3.148 ||| Numbered Discourses 3.148
15. Maṅgalavagga ||| 15. Good Fortune
Visamasutta ||| Unethical
“Tīhi, bhikkhave …pe… ||| “Someone with three qualities is placed in hell as if delivered there. …
visamena kāyakammena, visamena vacīkammena, visamena manokammena— ||| Unethical deeds by way of body, speech, and mind. …
imehi kho …pe… evaṁ niraye.
Tīhi, bhikkhave, dhammehi …pe… ||| Someone with three qualities is placed in heaven as if delivered there. …
samena kāyakammena, samena vacīkammena, samena manokammena— ||| Ethical deeds by way of body, speech, and mind. …”
imehi kho …pe… evaṁ sagge”ti.
Tatiyaṁ.
