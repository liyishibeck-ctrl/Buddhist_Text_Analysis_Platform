Aṅguttara Nikāya 5.248 ||| Numbered Discourses 5.248
25. Duccaritavagga ||| 25. Bad Conduct
Dutiyamanoduccaritasutta ||| Bad Mental Conduct (2nd)
“Pañcime, bhikkhave, ādīnavā manoduccarite …pe… ānisaṁsā manosucarite. ||| “Mendicants, there are these five drawbacks in bad mental conduct … benefits in good mental conduct …”
Katame pañca?
Attāpi attānaṁ na upavadati;
anuvicca viññū pasaṁsanti;
kalyāṇo kittisaddo abbhuggacchati;
asaddhammā vuṭṭhāti;
saddhamme patiṭṭhāti.
Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca ānisaṁsā manosucarite”ti.
Aṭṭhamaṁ.
