Aṅguttara Nikāya 3.46 ||| Numbered Discourses 3.46
5. Cūḷavagga ||| 5. The Lesser Chapter
Sīlavantasutta ||| Ethical
“Yaṁ, bhikkhave, sīlavanto pabbajitā gāmaṁ vā nigamaṁ vā upanissāya viharanti. ||| “Mendicants, when ethical renunciates are supported by a town or village,
Tattha manussā tīhi ṭhānehi bahuṁ puññaṁ pasavanti. ||| the people there brim with much merit in three ways.
Katamehi tīhi? ||| What three?
Kāyena, vācāya, manasā. ||| By way of body, speech, and mind.
Yaṁ, bhikkhave, sīlavanto pabbajitā gāmaṁ vā nigamaṁ vā upanissāya viharanti. ||| When ethical renunciates are supported by a town or village,
Tattha manussā imehi tīhi ṭhānehi bahuṁ puññaṁ pasavantī”ti. ||| the people there brim with much merit in these three ways.”
Chaṭṭhaṁ.
