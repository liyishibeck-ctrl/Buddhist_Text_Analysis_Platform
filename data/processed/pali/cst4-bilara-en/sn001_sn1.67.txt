Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.67 ||| Linked Discourses 1.67
7. Addhavagga ||| 7. Overcome
Uḍḍitasutta ||| Ensnared
“Kenassu uḍḍito loko, ||| “By what is the world ensnared?
kenassu parivārito; ||| By what is it surrounded?
Kenassu pihito loko, ||| By what is the world fastened shut?
kismiṁ loko patiṭṭhito”ti. ||| On what is the world grounded?”
“Taṇhāya uḍḍito loko, ||| “Craving has ensnared the world.
jarāya parivārito; ||| It’s surrounded by old age.
Maccunā pihito loko, ||| The world is fastened shut by mortality.
dukkhe loko patiṭṭhito”ti. ||| The world is grounded on suffering.”
