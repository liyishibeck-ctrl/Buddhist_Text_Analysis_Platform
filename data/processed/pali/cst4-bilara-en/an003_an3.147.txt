Aṅguttara Nikāya 3.147 ||| Numbered Discourses 3.147
15. Maṅgalavagga ||| 15. Good Fortune
Sāvajjasutta ||| Blameworthy
“Tīhi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ niraye. ||| “Someone with three qualities is placed in hell as if delivered there.
Katamehi tīhi? ||| What three?
Sāvajjena kāyakammena, sāvajjena vacīkammena, sāvajjena manokammena— ||| Blameworthy deeds by way of body, speech, and mind.
Imehi kho, bhikkhave, tīhi dhammehi samannāgato yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ niraye. ||| Someone with these three qualities is placed in hell as if delivered there.
Tīhi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ sagge. ||| Someone with three qualities is placed in heaven as if delivered there.
Katamehi tīhi? ||| What three?
Anavajjena kāyakammena, anavajjena vacīkammena, anavajjena manokammena— ||| Blameless deeds by way of body, speech, and mind.
imehi kho …pe… evaṁ sagge”ti. ||| Someone with these three qualities is placed in heaven as if delivered there.”
Dutiyaṁ.
