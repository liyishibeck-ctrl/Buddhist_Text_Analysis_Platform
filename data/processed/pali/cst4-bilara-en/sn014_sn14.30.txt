Saṁyutta Nikāya 14.30 ||| Linked Discourses 14.30
4. Catutthavagga ||| 4. Chapter Four
Catudhātusutta ||| Four Elements
Ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme … ||| At one time the Buddha was staying near Sāvatthī in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s monastery. …
“catasso imā, bhikkhave, dhātuyo. ||| “Mendicants, there are these four elements.
Katamā catasso? ||| What four?
Pathavīdhātu, āpodhātu, tejodhātu, vāyodhātu— ||| The elements of earth, water, fire, and air.
imā kho, bhikkhave, catasso dhātuyo”ti. ||| These are the four elements.”
Paṭhamaṁ.
