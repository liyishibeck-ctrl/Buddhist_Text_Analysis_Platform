Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.225 ||| Numbered Discourses 4.225
23. Duccaritavagga ||| 23. Bad Conduct
Paṭhamamaggasutta ||| Path (1st)
…pe… Micchādiṭṭhiko hoti, micchāsaṅkappo hoti, micchāvāco hoti, micchākammanto hoti …pe… sammādiṭṭhiko hoti, sammāsaṅkappo hoti, sammāvāco hoti, sammākammanto hoti …pe…. ||| “A foolish person … brims with much wickedness. … wrong view, wrong purpose, wrong speech, wrong action. … An astute person … brims with much merit. … right view, right purpose, right speech, right action. …”
Pañcamaṁ.
