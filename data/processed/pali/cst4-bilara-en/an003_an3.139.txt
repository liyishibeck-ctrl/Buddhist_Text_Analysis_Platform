Aṅguttara Nikāya 3.139 ||| Numbered Discourses 3.139
14. Yodhājīvavagga ||| 14. A Warrior
Vuddhisutta ||| Growth
“Tisso imā, bhikkhave, vuddhiyo. ||| “Mendicants, there are three kinds of growth.
Katamā tisso? ||| What three?
Saddhāvuddhi, sīlavuddhi, paññāvuddhi— ||| Growth in faith, ethics, and wisdom.
imā kho, bhikkhave, tisso vuddhiyo”ti. ||| These are the three kinds of growth.”
Sattamaṁ.
