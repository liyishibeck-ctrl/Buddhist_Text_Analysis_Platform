2. Dutiyapeyyālavagga ||| 2. Abbreviated Texts
Upaṭṭhitassatisutta ||| Mindful
“Upaṭṭhitassati ca hoti, paññavā ca hoti …pe… ||| “… They’re faithful, conscientious, prudent, mindful, and wise. …”
imehi kho, anuruddha, pañcahi dhammehi samannāgato mātugāmo kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā sugatiṁ saggaṁ lokaṁ upapajjatī”ti.
Navamaṁ.
Ime aṭṭha suttantasaṅkhepā.
